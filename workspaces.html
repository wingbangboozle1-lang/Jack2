Why there are seven

The brief asks for parallel sessions to coordinate through COORDINATION.md rather than overwrite each other. In practice each session started from a nearly empty repository, built a complete workbook, and discovered the others only when pushing. Every time, the resolution was the same: preserve what already exists, publish the new analysis beside it, and record the conflict. The result is redundant but intact — and the disagreements between the analyses are themselves informative, because they show which conclusions are robust to method and which are artefacts of an assumption.
