Why an NNN lease is worth more than the building

A triple-net lease transfers the operating risks — property tax, insurance, roof and structure — to the tenant, so the owner receives a bond-like rent stream. Buyers price that stream on the tenant's credit and on the number of years left. That is why the same building with the same rent is worth materially more with twelve years of term than with four: the buyer is not buying the bricks, it is buying a contractual cash flow with a known end date.

Permitted-use and drive-thru risk

The value of this asset to a replacement tenant depends on the drive-thru entitlement, not just the building. A drive-thru that requires a new conditional-use permit can sit dark for a year or more, and City rules on drive-thrus have tightened over time. The workbook cannot verify the site's entitlement without the address, so the re-leasing case is modelled with a vacancy allowance and a re-leasing cost rather than assumed to be instant.
