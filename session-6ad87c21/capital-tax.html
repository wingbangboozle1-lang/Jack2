Money and tax

Capital markets and tax

The Treasury curve, the transaction-tax stack, Proposition 13 and the Section 1031 mechanics that decide the sale structure.

What the data says. Long money is expensive and the curve is steep at the long end: the 10-year is 4.86% and the 30-year 5.26%. The 10-year fell 45 basis points between 5 October and 6 November 2026 (5.31% → 4.86%), which is the kind of move that re-prices net-lease assets. Transaction taxes are unusually high in this jurisdiction, and the Section 1031 exchange is the only mechanism that avoids paying them twice.

Measure ULA in detail — the cliff that can cost more than the sale

The City of Los Angeles imposes a base real property transfer tax of 0.45% and, for closings after 30 June 2026, a Measure ULA surtax of 4.0% above $5,400,000 and 5.5% at or above $10,900,000. Because the surtax applies to the consideration for the whole property rather than the excess over the threshold, a sale priced just above $5.4m pays 4.0% on everything. The thresholds are re-indexed every year to chained CPI, so a price that is under the threshold this year may be over it next year. S10 ↗

Practical consequence. If the modelled value of this asset is anywhere near $5.4m, the difference between a $5.35m sale and a $5.45m sale can be about $218,000 of tax on $100,000 of extra price. That is a structuring question for the sale, and it needs professional advice — but it belongs in the plan from day one, not at the closing table.

Section 1031, precisely

  • Both the relinquished and the replacement property must be held for business or investment use. Real property is like-kind to other real property inside the United States. S12 ↗
  • 45 calendar days from closing to identify replacement property in writing, signed and delivered to a person involved in the exchange. Notice to your own attorney or broker is not sufficient.
  • 180 calendar days to close, or the tax-return due date if earlier. The limits cannot be extended for hardship except after a presidentially declared disaster.
  • A qualified intermediary must hold the proceeds; you cannot be your own facilitator, and neither can your agent of the past two years.
  • Gain is deferred, not forgiven: basis carries over, so the tax is eventually paid. That is why the model taxes the 2040 liquidation in every strategy, including the exchange ones.
