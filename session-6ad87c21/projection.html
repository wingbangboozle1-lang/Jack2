How the numbers were produced

Projection system

A deterministic 2026–2040 model, a seeded Monte Carlo, a sensitivity grid and the break-evens — with every assumption published.

This page is the whole projection system: the inputs, the two independent engines (a deterministic 2026–2040 model and a seeded Monte Carlo), the sensitivity grid, and the break-evens. Everything is expressed per $100,000 of in-place annual net operating income, so no private rent figure is needed and the owner can scale any output by (actual NOI ÷ 100,000).

The equations

value(t) = NOI(t) ÷ cap(t)

cap(t) = base_cap + min(1.50%, 0.25% × (10 − remaining_term(t))) + drift × (t − 2026)

sale_proceeds = value − (4.375% + transfer_tax) × value − income_tax_on_gain

transfer_tax = 0.45% (≤ $5.4m) | 4.45% ($5.4m–$10.9m) | 5.95% (≥ $10.9m)

certainty_equivalent = [ E( u(wealth) ) × (1−γ) ] ^ (1/(1−&gamma)), γ = 2

That is the entire model. There is no machine learning, no proprietary data and no hidden adjustment. Every parameter is in the table below and every one can be replaced with a real figure from the lease, an appraisal or a CPA.

What the model cannot do

  • It cannot price the subject parcel. It prices a dollar of NOI. Substitute the real NOI to get a real number, and get an appraisal before relying on the level.
  • It cannot know the lease. The expiry, options, escalations, percentage rent and guaranty are unknown; the expiry window is tested at both ends.
  • It cannot see the franchisee. A franchisee balance sheet, a personal guaranty or a letter of credit would change the credit spread, and therefore the cap rate, materially.
  • It cannot forecast rates. The Treasury curve is treated as a fact and a range, not a forecast.
  • It does not model a land redevelopment alternative, which may dominate everything else if the site is under-improved.
