Nothing here is a criticism of another session's work: every one of these is a normal consequence of parallel authorship, and each has a stated fix.

4. What this means for relying on the site

  • The direction is robust; the levels are not. The ranking of strategies survives every sensitivity tested. The dollar amounts depend on a cap rate that nobody has verified, so treat them as ratios, not appraisals.
  • Three facts would change the answer most: the exact lease expiry and options, the tenant entity and guaranty, and the in-place rent against market. All three are obtainable this week.
  • The privacy control is absolute. No address, APN, owner name, rent, basis or loan figure is in this repository or on this site. If one appears, it is a defect and should be reported immediately.
