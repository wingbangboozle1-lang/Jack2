Decision-critical findings to review

A targeted audit of the root landing and the model it recommends. These findings do not verify the entire repository or determine the value of the property.

Decision gate: Do not choose to sell or renew based on the illustrative model rankings. Preserve both options while a qualified adviser privately verifies the lease, rights, deadlines, current market offers, property condition, insurance, taxes and the owner's objectives. No personal or parcel information is requested or stored here.
