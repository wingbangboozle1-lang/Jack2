A decision, not a prediction

Decision is conditional, not a property-specific valuation. The lease, actual NOI, tenant guarantee, tax basis and parcel location have not been verified. Do not use invented cap-rate cutoffs as a sell signal.

Executive summary

Start a dual track: privately review lease and tenant credit while seeking a credible renewal and independent sale offers before expiry. Compare after-tax, risk-adjusted proceeds with a documented renewal and realistic hold scenario. If renewal terms and credit are attractive, consider holding or selling with the extension; if not, compare a pre-expiry sale with re-letting or redevelopment. No option can be declared best for this individual on the available facts.

City and metropolitan indicators provide context, not a site appraisal. See the market table, transparent sensitivity, and missing facts and verification limits.
