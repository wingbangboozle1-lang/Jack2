How to use the model responsibly

Privately verify annual cash flows, rent steps and expenses; model each decision year by year, including vacancies, capex, insurance, debt and taxes. Stress a range of resale prices and discount rates and compare after-tax proceeds over the same horizon. A normalized sensitivity is not a probability or reliable forecast; unsupported probabilities and parcel-specific values remain blank.
