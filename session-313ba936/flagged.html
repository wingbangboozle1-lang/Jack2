Blanks, irregularities and what to obtain

Nothing here blocks a decision on the direction of travel. Everything here must be resolved before capital moves.

This page is the most important one on the site. It contains every place where this workbook does not know something, every place where the repository disagrees with itself, and every assumption that could be wrong in a way that changes the answer.

F01highTwelve prior workbooks in this repository reach different conclusions from overlapping, partly assumed inputsThe repository contains twelve independent session workbooks (session-1944e5a3 through session-f4f700c0, plus docs/). They use different access dates, different source sets, different rent assumptions and different cap-rate assumptions, and several present dollar-valued recommendations as if the rent and cap rate were known. F02mediumThree methodology errors were found and fixed inside this session's own model, and are disclosed rather than hidden(1) The first draft compared a growing asset against a non-growing yield, which mechanically favours holding by the entire growth term. It was rebuilt so that both strategies run through the same cash-flow engine with the same escalation and exit cap. (2) The first Monte Carlo applied a rate shock by lowering the reinvestment yield while raising the cap rate, double-penalising the sell path; a rate shock must raise reinvestment yields as well as cap rates. Both are visible in the git history of session-313ba936/scripts/model.py.None outstanding. Disclosed because a model that silently corrects itself is less trustworthy than one that shows its corrections.

Using it for a single central-LA drive-through is a labelled assumption, not a valuation.Obtain a broker opinion of value or an appraisal. Then read the sensitivity table on the Valuation page, which shows the answer at eight cap rates from 4.8% to 8.3%.

F06mediumThe local forecast horizon problem: official projections are used, but no local forecast covers the full lease windowCalifornia Department of Finance projects population and K-12 enrollment to 2035-36 and 2070, which does cover the expiry window and is used directly. Employment projections at the county level were not retrieved this session (row J17 is blank). The workbook therefore does not claim a local economic forecast for 2030-2035; it uses official demographic projections and the verified current labour-market series, and treats the rest as scenario assumptions.If a forward employment view is required, retrieve the EDD long-term industry and occupational projections directly rather than relying on a third-party forecast.
F07mediumSix stale pull requests from earlier sessions remain open and were deliberately not touchedPRs #5, #6, #10, #14, #18 and #30 target branches from earlier sessions, some more than four days old, and several contain work that has already been superseded. F08lowCensus QuickFacts reports several city-level business fields as unavailable while the county field is populatedOn the QuickFacts city/county table, the business statistics (employer establishments, total employment, payroll, firm ownership counts) carry an 'X' flag for the City of Los Angeles column while showing values for the county. The county values are used; no city business figure is published. Rows C06 and C07 are county-only for this reason.None. Recorded so a reader does not assume the city column was overlooked.
F09mediumTwo official sources cited by earlier sessions returned 404 on the access date and were not usedSeveral earlier workbooks cite a USGS FAQ URL for the 60%/46%/31% earthquake probabilities and a Los Angeles County Registrar-Recorder transfer-tax page. On 2026-11-10 the USGS FAQ URL returned a 404 error page and the Registrar page returned 'The resource you are looking for has been removed'. This workbook therefore cites the USGS Fact Sheet PDF that actually contains Table 1 (S18) and the Revenue and Taxation Code that authorises the county tax (S11) instead.This is a citation defect in the prior workbooks, not a data defect - the values themselves are correct. F10lowThe scored decision ranking depends on weights the analyst choseThe four strategies are scored 1-5 on wealth, growth, liquidity, risk and tax, with weights of 30/25/15/20/10. Those weights reflect the owner's stated priorities in the brief, but they are a judgement. A different weighting can change the ranking of the second and third strategies, though not the first.The weights are published on the Decision page so the owner can re-weight them. The recommendation's direction of travel rests on the breakevens and the tail asymmetry, not on the scorecard.
F11lowBusiness-interruption and pandemic-risk treatment is qualitative onlyThe model prices property damage from earthquake and fire on the hold path. It does not price business interruption, rent abatement during closure, or a pandemic-type demand shock as a separate stochastic process, because no official parameter exists for any of them at this level. They are recorded as risks R16 and R20 and mitigated contractually, not modelled.At renewal, negotiate a rent-abatement cap and confirm business-interruption coverage.

Documents to pull, in priority order

PriorityDocumentWhy it matters
1The lease and every amendmentExpiry, options, notice dates, escalations, recovery structure, named tenant, guaranty. Fourteen fields depend on this one document.
2The insurance declarations and a current quoteProperty, fire, earthquake, deductible, carrier appetite. The single input most likely to reverse the answer, and entirely absent today.
3A broker opinion of valueThe going-in cap rate. One market number converts every breakeven here into dollars.
4The franchisee's financial statementsRent coverage, unit-level P&L, guaranty capacity. The parent's filings are not the franchisee's credit.
5The franchise agreementTerm, development obligations, closure rights. Determines whether the tenant has a reason to extend.
6Loan documents and payoffBalance, rate, prepayment penalty or defeasance. Changes the net proceeds of any sale.
7Depreciation schedules and basisThe only way to move from pre-tax to after-tax wealth.
8Property condition report and Phase IDeferred maintenance and environmental. Both are buyer diligence items that reprice a deal.
9Title reportEasements, REAs, and anything that limits re-tenanting.
10Zoning and drive-through entitlement statusA legal non-conforming use is worth less to a successor tenant than a conforming one.
