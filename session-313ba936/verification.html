Verification

What was checked, and what each check actually proves

A passing link check is not evidence that a number matches its source. This page separates the two.

The distinction that matters. Automated checks can prove that a link resolves, that no page is orphaned, and that no blank row carries a value. They cannot prove that a number was copied correctly. That is why every source below records the exact text that was on the page, and why the build refuses to render a value that has no source.

What each check does NOT prove

  • A link check does not prove the value. V07 proves a URL resolves for the reader today. It cannot prove the number beside it was transcribed correctly. Only the human read recorded in verified_text does that.
  • A PII scan does not prove privacy. V09 is a regex. It catches formatted emails, phone numbers, street addresses and parcel patterns. It cannot catch an identifier written in an unusual form, so privacy is also enforced by construction: the repository never asks for or stores the address or APN.
  • A reproduction check does not prove the model is right. V11 proves the output is deterministic. It says nothing about whether the assumptions are defensible. That is what the assumptions table is for.
  • A closed-form cross-check does not prove the cash-flow engine. V12 proves one survival condition agrees with a formula. It does not validate the terminal value or the hazard process.
