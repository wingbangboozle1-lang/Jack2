Why this matters more than any single year’s jobs number. A restaurant lease runs for years. If the county loses roughly 216,000 residents between 2025 and 2040 while the state’s projection for 2070 is 8.41 million, then the long-run demand base for a suburban-format drive-through in a land-constrained metro is flat to declining. That does not make the site unviable — traffic and frontage can still carry it — but it removes the "growth will bail it out" argument from the hold case.

School enrollment: the clearest forward-looking indicator

The California Department of Finance projects Los Angeles County to lose 240,800 public K-12 students between 2025-26 and 2035-36 — the largest decline of any county in the state. Statewide enrollment has already fallen for nine consecutive years.
