No value on this site is the property’s value. The contract rent, the exact expiry date, the escalations, the owner’s basis and the debt are all unknown. Every dollar figure is therefore expressed per $100,000 of annual rent, and the decision is expressed as a breakeven the owner can test against a real appraisal. Fourteen owner-specific fields are blank on purpose and listed on Flagged for review (48 blank rows in total).

What the evidence says

The county is shrinking, officially

County population is 9,853,435 in 2025 and the state projects 9,637,524 by 2040 — a decline of 215,911 people. Census already shows −3.2% since 2020.

Read the evidence · source S03

The tenant is contracting

Jack in the Box same-store sales were −1.1% in Q3 FY2026, the system closed 17 restaurants in the quarter and the estate is 53 units smaller than a year ago under a named closure programme.

Read the evidence · source S15

Jobs are growing, but narrowly

County nonfarm employment rose 0.4% over the year, and 83% of the net gain was health care and social assistance. Information lost 10,200 jobs; restaurants gained 7,300 in the same period.

Read the evidence · source S06

The net-lease benchmark is 6.8%

The most recent official filing from a major net-lease buyer reports a 6.8% initial weighted average cash yield on Q3 2026 acquisitions, against a 4.88% 10-year Treasury.

Read the evidence · source S17

The hazard is real and local

USGS puts the Los Angeles region at 60% for a M6.7+ earthquake in 30 years. The Eaton Fire burned 14,021 acres inside this county in January 2025 and destroyed 9,419 structures.

Read the evidence · source S18

Selling is taxed, holding is capped

City transfer tax is 0.45%, county 0.11%, and Measure ULA adds 4% above $5.4m. Holding instead keeps the Proposition 13 assessed-value cap at 2% a year, which a sale resets.

Read the evidence · source S10

The recommendation in one paragraph

Open an extension negotiation now, and use the outcome to decide. On verified inputs, holding and selling land within a few percent of each other on central tendency (+4.9% of wealth), so the decision does not turn on a forecast — it turns on the rent and term the owner can negotiate and on how much concentrated tail risk the owner is willing to carry. A renewal concession of just 5.7% flips the answer toward selling, and an extension at a rent reset makes both the hold case and the sale price better. So the sequence is: read the lease, get the insurance quote, get an appraisal, negotiate the extension, then sell into a section 1031 exchange only if the implied cap rate is below the breakeven or the replacement clears the required yield.
