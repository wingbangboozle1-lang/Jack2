Why the exact expiry date is not a detail

The brief gives a window of 2030 to 2035. That five-year spread is the difference between a lease with four years left, which a buyer discounts heavily for rollover risk, and one with nine years left, which a buyer prices as stable income. It also determines whether the owner has time to negotiate before the tenant holds the option, and whether a section 1031 exchange can be executed without pressure. The model uses 2032 as the midpoint and says so; the owner should replace it with the real date before relying on any figure.

Who is actually on the hook for the rent

Jack in the Box operates 149 restaurants and franchises 1,966. Most likely the lease is with a franchisee entity, not with Jack in the Box Inc. That distinction is worth more than any cap-rate discussion: a franchisee has no access to the parent’s balance sheet unless a guaranty says so. The identity and financial strength of the operating franchisee, and the guaranty structure, are both blank in this workbook because they are only discoverable from the lease and the franchisee’s own statements.
