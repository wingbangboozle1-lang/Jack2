Privacy

What is deliberately not collected

Personal and parcel-identifying information is kept out of this repository by construction, not by redaction.

The rule

No personal information about the owner, and no information that would identify the property, appears in this repository or on this site. This is enforced in three ways, in increasing order of strength.

  1. A regex scan runs in scripts/verify.py over every page and data file, looking for email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses and parcel-number patterns. It is the weakest of the three, because a regex can only catch formats it knows.
  2. The dataset has no field for them. There is no column for an owner name, a street address, an APN, a loan number or an account number. There is a row that says the APN is deliberately not collected. You cannot leak a field that does not exist.
  3. The research method avoids them. Every source in this workbook is a published aggregate: a county, a city, a metro, a portfolio, a region. No lookup was performed at an individual parcel, and no address was ever entered into this session.

What is published instead of a property identifier

  • The asset is described as a single-tenant net-lease restaurant in central Los Angeles. That is the level of specificity the analysis needs and the level the brief supplies.
  • Money is expressed per $100,000 of annual rent. This is what makes it possible to publish a full decision analysis without ever publishing the rent.
  • Parcel-level facts that the owner needs — fire zone, flood zone, seismic screening, assessed value — are listed as lookups to perform on Flagged for review, with the official reference for each, rather than filled in.

Source-side note

The official pages this workbook reads are themselves public records. Two of the SEC pages carry the filing company's public headquarters address, which is a corporate disclosure, not personal information. It is not reproduced in this site's own text: the source table links to the filing and does not restate its contents beyond the financial figures needed.

If you are the owner

Do not send personal or parcel-identifying information into a chat or an issue. Put the numbers into your own copy of the model, or hand the documents to your accountant, appraiser and broker. This workbook was written so that it never needs to see them.
