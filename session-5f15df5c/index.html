Executive summary · decision readiness

Keep the options open. Do not decide “sell vs. renew” from the information currently available.

The known facts are only an anonymized Jack in the Box NNN property described as “around central Los Angeles” and a lease expiry window of 2030–2035. The site address, exact expiration, rent, options, NOI, debt, basis, hazards, insurance, and owner objectives are not provided. Therefore, a property-specific valuation or defensible final sale/renewal recommendation cannot be made without inventing inputs.

Best current course of action

Run a private, time-boxed dual track: (1) have counsel abstract the lease and calendar every notice deadline; (2) obtain independent valuation, insurance/hazard, condition, and tenant-credit diligence; (3) request a nonbinding extension proposal with documented rent reset/credit support while obtaining a sale opinion; (4) compare after-tax risk-adjusted proceeds only after the facts are known. Do not grant an extension, waive an option, list, or sign a sale/exchange solely on this public-area analysis.

Decision sequence

01 · Protect

Preserve legal and insurance options

Abstract the executed lease, verify exact term/notice dates, request current coverage and written renewal terms. Keep identifying documents private.

02 · Measure

Get independent facts

Establish actual NOI and tenant credit, then appraise as-is and under documented extension/vacancy cases. Screen parcel hazards and alternative-use constraints.

03 · Compare

Use owner-level, after-tax scenarios

Compare sell, extend, hold/renew, hold/vacate, and delayed-sale cases after transaction/tax costs, debt payoff, insurance and replacement investment risk.
