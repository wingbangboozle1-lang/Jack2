Material gaps and model limitations

These flags do not prevent using this site as a checklist. They do prevent treating prior precision or rankings as verified property advice.

ID / issueFinding and why it mattersRequired correction / reviewOfficial source
F01
Proxy rent shown in prior model		Root model input A1 is $150,000/year, calculated as 8% of Jack in the Box FY2025 average unit volume. The model itself labels it a placeholder, not subject rent. Any sale value or wealth comparison derived from it is not an appraisal or property estimate.Replace only with actual executed lease rent/NOI and reconciled expenses; keep prior output labelled illustrative.S10 ↗
F02
Expiry date replaced by midpoint		Root model selects 2032 as a midpoint of stated 2030–2035, although the executed expiry/options are unavailable. Timing changes rent duration, option deadlines, terminal value and buyer pricing.Use the executed lease and notice evidence; model each actual option/date, not a midpoint.S10 ↗
F03
Exit cap and renewal assumptions are analyst inputs		Prior projection code uses scenario cap rates, rent escalators, tenant renewal probabilities and renewal rent factors. These are not official published measurements of this unidentified site; model risk scores also self-identify as analyst judgments.Obtain subject-specific appraisals, lease and tenant evidence; show assumptions as ranges and avoid presenting scenario outputs as forecast probabilities.S10 ↗
F04
7.3% REIT yield is not a subject-property reinvestment quote		A public REIT’s acquisition yield (if cited) reflects its acquisitions and portfolio, not a guaranteed yield available to this owner or an equivalent replacement property.Get actual replacement-property proposals and compare net, after-tax, fee-adjusted, leverage-adjusted returns; retain taxable-sale alternative.S11 ↗
F05
Metro/county averages are not the property trade area		BLS/BEA statistics aggregate large geographies. Without the privately held location there is no way to select correct tract, school district, drive-time catchment, zoning jurisdiction or parcel hazard overlay.Geocode privately; disclose native geographic level and uncertainty; never print location or personal identifiers.S01 ↗
F06
City-specific tax eligibility is unconfirmed		Central LA does not prove the parcel is in Los Angeles City. City transfer tax/Measure ULA applicability and city planning maps depend on jurisdiction; rules may change before a sale.Verify municipality and current official transfer tax rules at closing; do not apply a City rate based only on the area label.S13 ↗
F07
Brand public filing does not answer franchisee/property questions		SEC company disclosures describe the issuer/system, not necessarily the private franchisee operating this unidentified unit, its guarantor, unit sales, lease or renewal choice.Obtain private tenant/guarantor financials and site-specific operating data under appropriate confidentiality controls.S10 ↗
F08
No parcel-level hazard result is possible here		No location is supplied; wildfire, flood, earthquake/liquefaction and insurance claims cannot be assigned based on metropolitan geography. A map check alone also does not equal engineering or insurance review.Perform private parcel screens at FEMA, CAL FIRE/local, and CGS; obtain engineer/broker review and confirm policy terms.S07 ↗
F09
No tax/after-tax decision can be calculated		Basis, depreciation, debt, ownership structure, tax status, exchange eligibility and replacement property are private and absent. Tax deferral is not the same as tax elimination.CPA/tax counsel uses confidential records and current IRS rules; publish only an anonymized conclusion if needed.S11 ↗
