Verification rules

  • Only primary official sources are used for factual observations: government statistical offices, regulators, official tax agencies, or SEC EDGAR.
  • A link is not proof that a site-specific value was established. The source's geographic and subject scope is stated beside every observation.
  • Unknown property-specific values are not filled by regional averages, analyst assumptions, brand averages or midpoint estimates.
  • Every data row in this workspace contains at least one official source link. Source files contain IDs, direct URL, scope and access date.
  • Private documents and owner details belong in confidential diligence—not this public repository.
