Pre-decision checklist

Questions, current answers, and how to close each gap

“Unknown” is intentional. Public regional data cannot answer private lease, parcel, tenant-credit, debt, tax-basis, insurance, or owner-objective questions. Every item has an official source link for context or the applicable public-data route.

Identity & jurisdiction		What is the exact parcel, legal description, and municipality?Unknown. ‘Central Los Angeles’ is not a parcel or a boundary determination.Needed: Privately held title report/APN; exact location retained off-site or in an owner-controlled private workspace.

Lease		What is the exact base-term end date, options, notice windows, and who controls each option?Unknown; only a 2030–2035 window is supplied.Needed: Executed lease, every amendment/side letter, option notices and delivery evidence.
Method: Commercial lease attorney prepares a date/notice abstract; calendar deadlines with counsel.		CriticalS10 ↗
Q03
Lease economics		What are current base rent, rent steps, reimbursements, percentage rent, and other income?Unknown; no rent roll or executed lease terms supplied.Needed: Current rent roll and 3–5 years of payment/reconciliation records.
Method: Reconcile bank ledger, lease invoices, and tenant statements privately; exclude PII from public site.		CriticalS10 ↗
Q04
Lease economics		Is the NNN obligation enforceable in practice, and what landlord costs leak through?Unknown; ‘NNN’ label alone does not quantify non-recoverable costs or exclusions.Needed: Lease expense clauses, tax/insurance/CAM reconciliation, owner-paid invoices, capital-responsibility clauses.
Method: Attorney/CPA reconcile lease language against actual cash expenses, separately identify structural/capital obligations.		CriticalS06 ↗ · S10 ↗
Q05
Renewal		How is option/renewal rent set (fixed, CPI, fair market value, appraisal/arbitration), and is there a floor or cap?Unknown; the option-rent mechanism is decisive and must not be inferred from brand-level filings.Needed: Option and rent-reset clauses; comparable evidence if market-rent provision applies.
Method: Counsel abstract first; obtain independent QSR/NNN market rent opinion and resolve appraisal process well before notice deadline.		CriticalS10 ↗
Q06
Tenant credit		Which legal entity is tenant, who guarantees it, and what is the guarantor’s recourse/strength?Unknown. A public-company 10-K is not a substitute for the private franchise operator’s financials or guaranty.Needed: Lease signature pages, guaranties, entity status, consent/assignment documents, tenant financials.
Method: Counsel verifies obligor chain; request confidential financial statements/credit package under NDA.		CriticalS10 ↗
Q07
Tenant operations		Is the restaurant open and trading normally, and what are unit sales, occupancy costs, and brand/territory conditions?Unknown; no unit-specific operating data supplied.Needed: Tenant-approved store sales/payment history, franchise status, notices/defaults, site-level operating reports.
Method: Request records via lease rights and counsel; do not publish store sales or operator identifiers.		HighS10 ↗
Q08
Property condition		What deferred maintenance, code, environmental, access, parking, drive-through, or capital work is due?Unknown; no inspection or environmental report supplied.Needed: Property condition assessment, Phase I environmental report, permits, code notices, capital plan.
Method: Commission qualified independent professionals; reconcile repair responsibilities to lease.		HighS05 ↗
Q09
Taxes & debt		What are adjusted tax basis, depreciation, ownership structure, debt payoff, and sale/exchange tax outcomes?Unknown; personal tax and financial details must remain private; tax outcomes cannot be calculated from public market data.Needed: Basis/depreciation ledger, entity/trust records, debt statement, payoff/prepayment terms.
Method: CPA/tax counsel builds sale, taxable sale, 1031, and hold cash-flow cases using confidential records.		CriticalS11 ↗ · S06 ↗
Q10
Taxes & sale costs		Which city/county transfer taxes apply at closing, and what will thresholds/rules be then?Unknown until municipality, consideration, transaction structure, and closing date are known. Current guidance can change before sale.Needed: Parcel jurisdiction, projected conveyance value, current official tax rules at closing.
Method: Verify applicable jurisdiction and re-check official tax schedules with closing counsel immediately before disposition.		HighS12 ↗ · S13 ↗
Q11
Valuation		What is actual market value today and at extension/sale, supported by relevant closed transactions?Unknown; no appraisal, actual NOI, or site-level comparable sales provided.Needed: Independent appraisal and broker opinions using verified lease, NOI, property condition, and recent comparable transactions.
Method: Get two independent opinions; reconcile subject NOI and cap-rate comps. Treat public REIT yields as context, not subject pricing.		CriticalS10 ↗
Q12
Local demographics		What population, household income, poverty, age, and housing trends describe the actual trade area?Unresolved. Without location no census tract, block group, buffer, or drive-time catchment can be selected.Needed: Privately geocoded site coordinate and explicitly chosen trade-area definition.
Method: After private geocoding, extract matched ACS tract/block-group estimates with margins of error and adjacent-year trend; distinguish ACS estimates from counts.		HighS01 ↗ · S14 ↗
Q13
Jobs & economy		What are nearby employment centers, sector mix, commute flows, and job growth in the effective trade area?Only metro-wide BLS context is verified; no site-specific labor shed or employer catchment is known.Needed: Privately geocoded location; industry/job counts, commute flows and trend with defined geography.
Method: Join official BLS/BEA/Census sources at their native geographies; disclose boundary mismatch and avoid extrapolating metro growth to one site.		HighS02 ↗ · S03 ↗ · S14 ↗
Q14
Schools		Which school districts/schools serve the site’s actual daytime workforce and nearby households, and how are outcomes changing?Unknown; school geography and relevant population cannot be determined from ‘Central Los Angeles.’ Schools may be a weak or indirect proxy for a QSR property.Needed: Site location; attendance boundaries; current official enrollment/performance data.
Method: Verify attendance boundary with responsible district; use California DataQuest for school/district/county metrics and note ecological limitations.		MediumS04 ↗ · S05 ↗
Q15
Zoning & land use		What zoning, specific plan, entitlement, access, redevelopment, and nearby project changes affect the site?Unknown until municipality and parcel are identified.Needed: Parcel-level planning/zoning map, entitlements, permits, right-of-way and nearby project record.
Method: If inside Los Angeles City, check ZIMAS and confirm with Planning; otherwise use the actual jurisdiction’s official planning office.		HighS05 ↗
Q16
Natural hazard		What parcel-level wildfire, flood, earthquake, liquefaction, landslide, and extreme heat exposure applies?Unknown. County/metro averages cannot establish parcel exposure; maps are not a property engineering or insurance assessment.Needed: Private parcel location, current map layers, engineering/roof/drainage review, claims history.
Method: Screen FEMA NFHL, CAL FIRE/local FHSZ, and CGS; then obtain engineering and insurer review; record map date and uncertainty.		CriticalS07 ↗ · S08 ↗ · S09 ↗
Q17
Insurance		Can full replacement, liability, rent-loss, flood/earthquake and wildfire-related cover be renewed at affordable terms?Unknown; no policy, loss history, deductible or quote supplied.Needed: Current declaration pages, exclusions, renewal notice, loss runs, replacement-cost estimate, broker quotes.
Method: Have independent commercial broker obtain comparable written quotes; review exclusions and landlord/tenant split privately.		CriticalS07 ↗ · S08 ↗ · S09 ↗
Q18
Alternative use		If the tenant exits, can the site be relet to another QSR or converted, and at what cost/time?Unknown; no zoning, property condition, market-rent or tenant-demand evidence.Needed: Zoning/driveway/entitlement assessment, site condition, independent leasing study, tenant improvement budget.
Method: Ask land-use counsel and two local brokers for documented alternatives; test downside with long downtime and capital reserve.		HighS05 ↗
Q19
Owner objectives		What liquidity need, income requirement, concentration limit, time horizon, and loss tolerance should govern?Unknown and intentionally not requested for this public artifact; personal finances and preferences remain private.Needed: Private owner-defined investment policy, income/liquidity needs, diversification targets, estate/tax objectives.
Method: Owner and fiduciary adviser model anonymized scenarios locally; publish only non-identifying decision constraints if desired.		CriticalS11 ↗
Q20
Sale / exchange execution		If selling, are the specific replacement property, qualified intermediary, timing, and post-tax deployment plan actually viable?Unknown; a 1031 is not automatic tax exemption and no exchange eligibility or replacement investment is confirmed.Needed: Tax counsel opinion, QI engagement plan, property identification/closing dates, replacement asset diligence, liquidity plan.
Method: Tax professional confirms eligibility and deadlines from current IRS rules; underwrite alternatives net of fees, debt, taxes, and concentration risk.		HighS11 ↗
