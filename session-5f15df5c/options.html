Decision pathways

No path dominates without the missing facts.

The recommended immediate decision is to preserve optionality while gathering evidence—not a final sell/renew choice. Compare net present value, after-tax cash flow, liquidity, downside and concentration using the same horizon and risk tolerance.

Personal wealth balance

Asset-level return is not the same as owner-level suitability. A final choice should compare after-tax spendable cash, income reliability, liquidity, diversification, estate/tax constraints, leverage and ability to absorb correlated shocks. Personal balance-sheet and health information must stay private and is not required on this site.
