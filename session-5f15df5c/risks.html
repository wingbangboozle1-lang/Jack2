Correlated stress scenarios to model after diligence

  • Earthquake + insurance stress: damage or access disruption, a large deductible/exclusion, regional repair-cost spike, tenant interruption and lower buyer liquidity.
  • Wildfire smoke/evacuation + recession: store interruption and insurance changes arrive with weaker local household spending and lower sale prices.
  • Tenant closure + high rates: rent stops as cap rates expand and re-tenanting finance/tenant improvements become more costly.
  • Flood/access disruption + claim denial: physical damage and business interruption exceed mapped assumptions or applicable coverage.
  • Exchange failure + market fall: a missed statutory timeline or poor replacement purchase leaves tax liability and reinvestment losses; model taxable sale too.

No “black swan probability” is assigned. Before using risk scores, define exposure, vulnerability, consequence, insurance recovery, recovery time, and source vintage for the actual parcel.
