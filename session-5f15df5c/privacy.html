Privacy · highest priority

No personal or parcel-identifying information is published here.

Privacy protection takes precedence over making a parcel-level analysis look complete. Exact location is not needed to provide this public decision framework and is not requested here.

Public-site rule

Do not add personal names, home/business street address, APN, legal description, account/loan identifiers, tenant employee/contact details, private financial statements, or unredacted documents to site files, issues, chat, or commits. Use generic placeholders only. Store source documents with your attorney/adviser or in an owner-controlled private system, not GitHub Pages.

Safe private workflow

  1. Keep unredacted lease, title, tax, debt, financial, insurer and tenant-credit files off the public repo.
  2. Give each adviser only the documents needed under confidentiality controls. Have counsel abstract the lease without exposing personal details on public pages.
  3. For official parcel/hazard lookups, an owner/adviser can privately use the exact location on the official portals linked here. Publish only non-identifying result categories if necessary, with source, date, and caveat.
  4. Before any public commit, scan generated pages, assets, JSON, logs, branch/PR text and filenames. Remove identifiers rather than hashing them; a hash can still enable linkage.
  5. Ask legal/tax counsel before sharing tenant or financial information. Public-company disclosures are not a reason to publish private franchisee records.
