# Session 643e4aab — Central LA Jack in the Box (NNN): sell before expiry, or renew?

A source-verified decision workbook for a single-tenant Jack in the Box restaurant on a triple-net lease in
Central Los Angeles, lease expiry in the 2030–2035 window. Every figure is normalised to **$100,000 of NOI**.

## The answer, in one paragraph

Do not drift to expiry. The rent is the asset and the lease is what the rent is worth, so the decision that
controls the outcome is **remaining term**, not whether the corner is gentrifying. The model says holding to
expiry is worth more than a 1031 exchange today ($4.48m vs $4.21m per $100k of NOI, nominal 2040) and wins 67.2%
of 10,000 draws — but only because it assumes the tenant renews at market. A taxable sale never wins, because the
deferral is what makes an exit competitive at all. So: open a renewal conversation priced at a market reset, run a
sale in parallel priced on a 1031, and sell if the achievable price implies a cap rate at or below the **7.18%**
breakeven. The two reasons to sell anyway are concentration (one tenant, one parcel, one metro) and tail risk that
cannot be diversified inside one building (60% chance of an M6.7+ earthquake in the region within 30 years).

## Rules this workbook follows

- **Privacy.** The property address, assessor parcel number, owner identity and all contact details are withheld
  and replaced with `[REDACTED-PROPERTY-01]` / `[REDACTED-OWNER-01]`. The verifier scans every generated page for
  email, phone, SSN, parcel-number and street-address patterns and fails the build on a match. Nothing personal is
  published, and nothing personal is stored in this repository.
- **No invented values.** A value that could not be confirmed from an official source is left **blank**, marked
  `unverified`, and listed on the flagged page with the exact lookup that would close it. The engine refuses to
  accept a row that is not marked verified, so a blank can never silently become an assumption.
  Currently **25 of 129 rows are deliberately blank.**
- **One source per row.** Every row carries a source id that resolves to a real https URL in `sources.json`.
  Aggregators are not used for values. Where a source is an industry or analyst estimate rather than an official
  statistic, the risk register says so.
- **Legal and tax caveat.** This is decision support, not legal, tax or investment advice. The §1031 timing rules,
  the depreciation schedule and the applicable capital-gains tier all need a qualified intermediary and a CPA
  before any money moves.
