Taxes and exit costs

Statutory transfer taxes, the Measure ULA cliff, Proposition 13, and the mechanics of a 1031 exchange.

A sale in the City of Los Angeles is not a 5% transaction. Costs, transfer taxes and the tax on the gain are the reason a taxable sale scores last on every table in this workbook.

The Proposition 13 asymmetry

A hold keeps a 1% levy on an assessed value that can only rise 2% a year. A sale resets the assessed value to the purchase price and hands the buyer a permanently higher tax bill - which the buyer capitalises into a lower price. This is a real, structural cost of selling that does not appear on any closing statement.

The 1031 mechanism

Section 1031 applies only to real property held for business or investment. It defers - it does not forgive - the gain. The two hard constraints are a written identification within 45 days and receipt of the replacement property within 180 days, or by the tax return due date if that comes first S12 ↗. On the federal side the top long-term rate is 20%, with prior depreciation recaptured at up to 25% S13 ↗; California offers no preferential capital gains rate at all - all gains are taxed as ordinary income S14 ↗.
