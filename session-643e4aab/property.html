Why "2030-2035" is itself a finding

A buyer prices term. Two assets with identical rent, one with nine years remaining and one with four, do not trade at the same cap rate - the shorter one carries re-leasing risk, downtime and re-fitting capital. A five-year uncertainty band around the expiry therefore cannot be treated as a rounding error: it is the difference between selling a bond and selling a vacancy risk.

It runs the other way too. If the tenant holds renewal options, the tenant decides whether the lease continues, not the owner. The owner's "renew or sell" framing assumes a choice the owner may not have. The first question for the lease is not "when does it end" but "who controls what happens when it ends".
