Why so few sources, and why some are blank

Because the brief requires that a value be traceable to the agency that published it. That rules out the sources that would normally fill the remaining gaps: no government agency publishes single-tenant cap rates, parcel-level rent, a specific building's hazard class or an owner's basis. Where the official record is silent, the row stays blank. A confident number from a commercial listing service would be more useful-looking and less true, and this workbook is built the other way.

Two agencies, two populations

A caution that applies to the population data: the Census Bureau's July 1, 2025 estimate for Los Angeles County is 9,694,934, while the California Department of Finance's 2025 projection for the same county is 9,853,435. Both are official. They are not interchangeable, and the difference of 158,501 people is a methodological difference, not an error. This workbook uses Census for current estimates and DOF for the forward projection, and never mixes them in one calculation.
