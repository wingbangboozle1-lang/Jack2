How to read the credit

Negative stockholders' equity is a capital-structure condition, not an imminent default signal - the company has been profitable in fiscal 2026 to date, has reduced long-term debt from $1.603bn to $1.492bn, and is generating positive annual revenue of roughly $1.2bn on a nine-month run rate. The correct reading for a landlord is that the tenant can pay this rent, but that the tenant is a rational operator managing its own store portfolio, and this particular unit will survive on its own economics.

What is missing here matters more than what is present. The unit count, the average unit volume and the company's own statement about its renewal practice are all blank rows. Those figures sit in the narrative sections of the 10-K rather than in the machine-readable financial data, and this session did not re-read that narrative. They are left blank rather than carried over from another session's work, and they are the first thing to pull from the filing.
