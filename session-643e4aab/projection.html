Projection model

The verified inputs, every assumption, every formula, and a 10,000-trial simulation of the four decisions.

Every number on this site comes from one deterministic file plus one seeded simulation. Here is the whole thing: the verified inputs, every assumption, the formulas, the sensitivity tables, and the results.

The unit of account

Per $100,000 of current annual net operating income, in nominal 2040 dollars. The owner's actual rent was never provided, so publishing a dollar value for the property would be an invention. A multiple per $100k of NOI is a fact the owner can scale by their own rent. Every strategy is measured the same way: both the subject and any replacement asset are held as property, with growing rent, reinvested cash, and a sale at the horizon net of costs and tax.

Assumptions - all of them

No official source publishes asset-level cap rates, NNN rent growth, or the owner's basis. Those are assumptions, and they are listed here in full with the reason for each. None of them is presented as data anywhere on the site.

AssumptionValueWhy this value
cap_subject6.25% base; tested 4.50%-7.25%No official source publishes asset-level cap rates - metric C04 is deliberately blank. The base sits above the verified 4.84-4.90% 10-year Treasury plus a single-tenant risk premium. The band, not the point, is the answer.
cap_replacement6.50%The replacement asset bought with sale proceeds. Set 25 bp ABOVE the subject's cap so the sell path is not flattered by assuming the owner can buy the same yield with less concentration. Net-lease portfolios do trade at tighter caps than a single asset, so 6.50% is conservative.
growthBear 1.0% / Base 2.0% / Bull 3.7%Applied identically to the subject and to the replacement, so it cancels except where term differs. Bull equals the verified LA CPI-U year-over-year rate (W03), i.e. a fully indexed escalation. Base is a typical fixed NNN escalator, deliberately BELOW verified CPI so the base case does not flatter holding.
reset_uplift+8.0% at extensionMark-to-market at renewal. Set just above the breakeven the model computes, and stress-tested at 0% and 15%, so the recommendation is not created by this assumption.
renew_prob60%The tenant's own renewal practice could not be re-verified this session (metric N12 is blank). 60% is a neutral prior; the Monte Carlo draws 40%-85%.
vacancy_months / relet_discount9 months / -5%Downtime and a below-renewal re-let price if the tenant leaves. Typical for a purpose-built QSR box that must be re-permitted and re-fitted.
disposition cost3.00% brokerage + 0.50% closing + statutory transfer taxesBrokerage and closing are judgment. The taxes are the verified statutory stack: 0.11% county (S10), 0.45% City base (S11) and Measure ULA 4%/5.5% above $5.4M/$10.9M (S11).
basis_ratio / depreciated share / CA marginal55% / 30% / 13.3%The owner's basis, depreciation history and marginal rates were not provided and are withheld for privacy (metrics L08, T15). Stated only to model the MECHANICS of a taxable sale. They are not a statement of the owner's liability.
portfolio_yield5.50%Income yield on the 1031 replacement portfolio after transaction costs. Shown only in the liquidity illustration, not in the main strategies.
term_premium5% price premium when selling with long remaining termA buyer pays more for 9-10 years of term than for 3-4. Modelled as a 5% lower exit cap on the extend-then-sell path and stress-tested at 0% and 10%, so the result does not depend on this being right.
assessed_today$600,000Illustrative, to quantify the Proposition 13 differential. The actual assessed value was not provided.

The simulation

10,000 trials, fixed seed 20261107, so every figure is reproducible by running the engine. Ranges come from the published sensitivity band and the stated assumption ranges, not from fitted historical data, because no official series publishes asset-level cap rates or NNN rent growth.

VariableDistribution
cap_subjecttriangular 4.50% / 6.25% / 7.25%
cap_replacementnormal 6.50%, sd 0.60%, floored at 3.00%
noi_growthnormal 2.1%, sd 1.2%, floored at 0
reset_upliftuniform 0%-14%
renew_probuniform 40%-85%
expiry_yearuniform 2030-2035 (the stated window)
cash_reinvestnormal 6.50%, sd 1.00%, floored at 0
