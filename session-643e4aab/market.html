Market economics

Population, income, employment, wages, labour regulation, demand and schools - verified against official sources.

Every figure below was read from the official source on 2026-11-07. Where an agency publishes no value, the cell is blank rather than estimated.

Two things to notice before the tables. First, the labour market in the city has genuinely improved: unemployment is 4.5% and below the state average, and the metro is adding jobs faster than the nation. Second, the mix is shifting away from office employment and the state's own projection has the county losing population through 2040. Those two facts point in opposite directions, which is why this is a term-and-rent decision rather than a location bet.

What is deliberately not projected

No official agency publishes a projection of employment, household income, rent or cap rates for a single parcel or submarket. Population is projected by the state; that is the projection used. Everything else in the model is a declared assumption with a published sensitivity band, because a fabricated growth rate would look like data. Note also that the Bureau of Economic Analysis discontinued metropolitan-area statistics with its 2024 county release S20 ↗, so any metro-level income projection would have to come from somewhere other than the federal statistical system.
