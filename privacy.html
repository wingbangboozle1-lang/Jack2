About

Privacy

No personal or parcel-identifying information is collected, stored or published, and the build refuses to publish anything that looks like it.

Privacy was the first rule of the work. This page states what is not collected and how the build enforces it.

InformationHow it is handledHow the build enforces it
Owner's name, contact details, identityNever collected. The owner is [OWNER-01] throughout.Build stops if an e-mail address, telephone number or a known individual's name is about to be published.
Property address, parcel number, map locationNever collected. The property is [PROPERTY-01]. No parcel was looked up, because the lookup itself would identify it.Build stops on a street-address or parcel-number pattern.
Rent, lease terms, value, loan, tax basisLeft blank. They are listed with how to obtain them privately.Build stops if a blank row carries a value.
Names of individuals in official documentsReplaced with the placeholder [OFFICER] in the earlier folders (2026-11-13). Company and agency names are kept.Names seen during research are stored only as salted hashes; the build hashes every pair of adjacent capitalised words and stops on a match.
Tracking, cookies, outside scripts or fontsNone. The site is plain pages, one style sheet and one small script, all served from this repository.No address outside this site is loaded by any page.
Search within the siteThe filter boxes work in the reader's browser. Nothing typed is sent anywhere.The script has no network calls.
