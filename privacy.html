Privacy

No personal information is collected, stored, published or inferred

This is the highest-priority constraint in the brief, so it is enforced in code rather than promised in prose.

Summary. This repository and the published site contain zero personal identifiers. The property is referred to only as “the Subject Property (Central Los Angeles)”. There is no owner name, entity name, street address, parcel number, loan number, policy number, contact detail, or financial account anywhere. Values the brief did not supply are marked [WITHHELD - placeholder] and flagged.

What was deliberately excluded

CategoryTreatment
Owner identity and entity structureExcluded. Not requested, not inferred, not stored.
Street address and APNExcluded. The brief references only “central Los Angeles”. Parcel-level lookups are therefore flagged unverified (M82–M85).
Lease documentsNot published. Only the clause categories to audit are listed.
Tenant franchisee identityExcluded. Only the franchisor (a public company) is named, which is public record.
Financial accounts, loan numbers, policy numbersExcluded.
Correspondence and contact detailsExcluded. No form, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts on this site.

How it is enforced

  • At build time: tools/build_site.py scans every emitted page against patterns for email addresses, telephone numbers, US street addresses, and parcel numbers, and the build fails on a match.
  • In CI: tools/check_site.py re-runs the same scan plus link and source checks on every push.
  • By data design: data/property_lease.json carries placeholders, not values. There is no field in the schema for an owner name.
  • By scope: the workbook cites public companies (Jack in the Box Inc.), government statistics, and public filings only.

Internal-use classification

The market analysis, the model code, and the flagged list are internal decision support. If the owner later supplies the missing documents, those documents must stay outside this repository, or in a private repository — never in a published one. The public Pages site must remain free of them.

Client-side behaviour

The site is fully static. There is no server, no database, no form, no analytics, no cookie, no third-party font or script, and no outbound request other than the source links you choose to click. Search and filtering run entirely in your browser.

Internal decision support — not an appraisal, not tax advice, not investment advice. Every data value on this site links to the official source it was read from on 2026-11-07. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. No personal or parcel-identifying information is published.
