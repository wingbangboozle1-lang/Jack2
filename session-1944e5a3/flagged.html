C. Data-quality observations

  • Census QuickFacts tool fallback: the verification tool returned the national table for city/CDP selectors and the county table for the county URL — the county values were read directly and are trustworthy; city/CDP values could not be read and are therefore blank, not guessed (F-01/F-02).
  • Vintage drift: QuickFacts shows V2025 population estimates (through Jul 2025) alongside 2020–2024 ACS for income/housing — mixing vintages is per Census conventions but noted.
  • Source vintage: LA County unemployment for "2025 average" (5.7%, not SA) and Sep 2026 (4.8% SA) use different concepts (annual not-SA vs monthly SA) — labeled throughout.
  • BEA release timing: 2024 county GDP released Feb 2026; BEA discontinued metro-area GDP series — county is the unit used here.
  • LAEDC vs EDD: LAEDC projected 6.1% UR for 2025; BLS/EDD show 2025 actual 5.7% county (EDD Sep 2026: 4.8% SA) — projections run hot vs estimates; treat LAEDC as scenario bounds, not actuals.
