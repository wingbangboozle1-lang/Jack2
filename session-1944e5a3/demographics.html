Why schools matter for a NNN asset. School enrollment and district quality drive household formation and family trade-area stability for QSR pads. LAUSD decline is a headwind for family-oriented formats, partly offset by the district's scale and by strong independent/charter ecosystems in central LA. This factor is secondary to rent, cap rate and tenant credit — but it favors locations near stable enrollment or employment centers.
