Key findings

  • The county is big and growing slowly. GDP ≈ $1.00T (2024); payroll employment 4.54M (Sep 2026, +0.4% YoY). Growth is real but modest; LAEDC projects +2.1% real GDP 2025 and +1.3% 2026.
  • Population is flat-to-declining (−3.2% 2020→2025, county), so value must come from income density, redevelopment upside, and DTLA 2040 zoning capacity — not from raw headcount growth.
  • Downtown has an official growth plan. DTLA 2040 (adopted 2023, finalized Dec 2024) targets +125,000 residents, +70,000 housing units, +55,000 jobs by 2040 — a long-run tailwind for DTLA retail and land value.
  • Tenant credit is the pivot. Jack in the Box is 93% franchised, divested Del Taco (~$115M), and discontinued its dividend while paying down debt — a NNN landlord should underwrite the franchisee, not just the brand.
  • Fire risk is real but geographically specific. January 2025 Palisades/Eaton fires destroyed ~16,000 structures (UCLA: $95–164B losses); Central LA is urban core — fire risk sits mostly with insurer sentiment, not direct burn probability. Earthquake (Puente Hills thrust beneath downtown) is the principal black-swan for this parcel.
  • Selling is expensive in this city. Measure ULA adds 4% on sales >$5.4M and 5.5% ≥$10.9M (plus 0.45% city + county transfer tax). Tax drag materially favors hold-and-renew or a 1031 exchange over a ULA-taxed outright sale.

Recommendation (balanced, trigger-based)

Renew first — sell only on defined triggers. Negotiate a 10–15 year extension with rent escalations and credit protection now (2026–2027), while silently preparing a confidential sale process as a fallback. Do not let the lease run to 2030–2035 without an extension. Sell only if a trigger fires (page “Sell vs Renew”) — e.g., renewal fails, tenant credit materially deteriorates, or an offer prices the asset at/above replacement economics.
  • Best for wealth preservation: renew on strong terms — predictable, escalating NNN income, DTLA 2040/2028-Olympics tailwinds, avoids ULA drag and 1031 timing risk.
  • Acceptable growth path: if offer ≥ target (≈ market cap-rate math in Projection page) or 1031 replacement already identified, sell with a qualified intermediary — never take constructive receipt.
  • Avoid: letting the lease lapse unextended (value reset on renewal), panic-selling into weak retail cap rates, and concentrated re-tenancy into an unproven operator.
  • Risk budget: target ≤ 15–20% of investable net worth in this single asset; keep 12–18 months of carry reserve; verify insurance/earthquake coverage annually.
