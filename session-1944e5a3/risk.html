Reading the register — what actually threatens the outcome

  • The #1 controllable risk is BAU-03 (cap-rate/rate risk) — it swamps everything else and is why panic-selling is the cardinal sin here.
  • The #1 verifiable credit risk is BAU-01 — JIB's system is 93% franchised and AUV is falling; your real counterparty is a franchisee balance sheet.
  • The #1 catastrophic tail is BS-01 — rare × ruinous. It does not justify selling (risk is city-wide, price reflects it), it justifies verified insurance and retrofit checks.
  • Wildfire (BS-03) is an insurance-pricing story for this parcel, not a burn-probability story — Central LA is urban core; the FHSZ map shows very-high zones concentrated in the hills/brush interface (Palisades, Altadena), not the urban core. Verify parcel zone at CAL FIRE's official map.
  • Aggregate non-catastrophic value at risk if renewed 2026→2035 (rough, illustrative): ≈10–25% of asset value across BAU items; aggregate tail risk mitigated to insurance-deductible levels with proper coverage.
Non-negotiables. (1) Earthquake + fire coverage with loss-of-rents, re-marketed annually. (2) Estoppel + franchisee financials before any extension or sale. (3) No sale without a 1031 plan if embedded gains are material. (4) This asset ≤ 15–20% of investable net worth. (5) Review this register annually each Q1 and 12 months before lease expiry.
