Verification protocol (how "verified" was applied)

  • Direct official read: figures read in the official source during this build (Census QuickFacts county table, BLS metro release, EDD county PDF, BEA release, SEC 10-K, Port of LA releases, LA City Clerk/LAHD PDFs) are marked Verified with inline links.
  • Mirror read: FRED is a Federal Reserve Bank mirror of BLS/BEA series — accepted as official for series values; primary releases linked where applicable.
  • Reported: values visible only in secondary write-ups of official documents (e.g., StockTitan's 10-K summary) are marked Reported and always linked to both.
  • Computed: arithmetic derivations (e.g., −3.8% AUV, +2.1% GDP, weighted scorecard, scenario values) are marked Computed with the formula; they inherit the source's reliability.
  • Unverified: anything not confirmable from an official source in this build was left blank or marked Flagged — see Flagged for Review. No values were invented.
