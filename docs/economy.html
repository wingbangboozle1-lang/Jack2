Local economy & demographics

Central Los Angeles: a deep, mature, shrinking market

The brief asked for population growth, income levels, school systems, job growth and every factor that could influence the local economy. The verified answer contradicts the usual assumption: population is falling and job growth has been flat for seven years. Every row below links to its official source.

Underwrite zero real NOI growth. This market is deep enough to keep a well-located QSR box productive, and too stagnant to justify holding a decaying lease for a premium.

The most important economic finding in this dossier. The Los Angeles metro has added 45,400 jobs in seven years — 0.10% per year. With a labour force of roughly five million, that is effectively full stall for most of a decade, and employment is now 1.2% below its October 2024 peak.

The unemployment rate of 4.5% in September 2026 looks healthy in isolation — and is back to its 2019 level. Read alongside a declining population and flat payrolls, the benign interpretation is that people have left the labour force, not that hiring is strong.

QSR revenue is roughly traffic × ticket × capture rate. A county that has shed 322,480 residents is a county shedding customers, before any price effect. This is a mature, slowly contracting market — not a growth market.

Income, affordability and the customer

A $90,112 county median household income looks strong until it is set against an $834,200 median home value — a ~9.3× price-to-income ratio — and $1,954 median monthly rent. Households in the lower half of the distribution are structurally squeezed, and those households are the Jack in the Box customer. JACK's own management attributed FY2025 sales weakness to lower-income and Hispanic consumers pulling back.

Spending depth

Los Angeles County recorded $213.9 billion of retail sales in 2022 — $22,010 per resident — and $44.0 billion of accommodation and food services sales. That is a deep market: a single unit doing $1.5–2.5 million is a rounding error, which is why a departing tenant can eventually be replaced. It also means the market will not grow the box for you.

Education

36.0% of county adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher — above the national figure, and positive for daytime professional traffic. School quality is deliberately not assessed: the assigned attendance area cannot be determined without the address, so no claim is made. For a QSR the more relevant education-linked number is household formation, which is why the 19.2% under-18 share is reported.
