The four facts that matter most

1 · Corporate vs franchisee is ~100 bp

5.85% vs 6.85%
If PROP-001's tenant is a franchisee without a corporate guaranty, its pricing anchor is the 6.85% line — not 5.85%. That is roughly 15% of value.

2 · Term is worth a lot

~105 bp
7-Eleven prices 5.90–6.20% at 5 years and 4.80–5.15% at 15 years. Identical credit, ~17% more value for the same NOI, purely from term.

3 · Weakness is repriced brutally and fast

8.50–8.90%
Family Dollar moved from investment grade to unrated on its sale and repriced immediately. Burger King widened 25 bp at 10-year. The market does not wait for confirmations.

4 · The market is bifurcating, not falling

25 bp
Bid–ask spreads in retail widened to 25 bp in Q3 2026. Premium long-term credit trades tight; short-term and unrated product trades wide and selectively.

PROP-001 sits on the wrong side of that line today — short-term and probably unrated — and moves further out on it every year it is not extended. That is the entire valuation argument in one sentence.
