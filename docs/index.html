Read the shape, not just the average. "Renew and hold" wins most often (52.6%) and has the highest median. It also has a worst-decile outcome of 1.88× NOI against 10.96× for selling via §1031 — five times worse in the bad state. That is what a single parcel, single tenant, single metro bet looks like. The brief asked for manageable risk and wealth preservation, so the risk-adjusted ranking governs, and selling via §1031 wins it.

Five findings that drive the answer

01 · Time destroys value mechanically

−22.7%
Value per $1 of NOI falls from $12.48 today to $9.64 once the lease is inside its final year. No rent change, no tenant change, no market change — just the passage of time. Every month the lease runs down, that is given away for free.

02 · A signature is worth more than a rate cut

+25.2%
Extending from 6.15 years to ~15 years moves the model cap rate from 8.02% to 6.40%: the same NOI goes from $12.48 to $15.62 per $1 — about $629,873 on a $200,000-NOI asset.

03 · The trade area will not bail it out

+0.10%/yr
LA metro payrolls have grown 0.10% a year for seven years and are 1.2% below their October 2024 peak. The county has lost 322,480 residents since 2020. Underwrite zero real NOI growth.

04 · The tenant is retrenching

2,168 → 2,115
Jack in the Box's store count fell across four quarters, with 51 closures in a branded block-closure programme. Del Taco was bought for ~$585m and sold for ~$119m. A corporate guaranty is the most valuable fact not yet known.

05 · Rates are moving against holding

4.06% → 5.13%
The 10-year Treasury's monthly average rose 107 bp in twelve months; the Fed hiked on 2026-09-17; the 30-year mortgage is 7.18%. Net-lease cap rates rose to 6.92% in Q3 2026 and the market is "resetting to a higher cost of capital rather than waiting for relief."

The $500,000 phone call

$500,000
Moving the assumed expiry from 2030 to 2035 changes value from $11.57 to $14.07 per $1 of NOI — a 21.6% swing. Reading the lease costs nothing and is worth more than any decision in this dossier.
