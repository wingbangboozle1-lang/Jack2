Tax & personal wealth

The tax tail should not wag the decision

A sale at today's value would trigger roughly 23.3% of the price in combined federal and California tax under the model's assumptions. That is a real number and it is the main reason Owners hold. It is also smaller than the cost of doing nothing.

A §1031 exchange defers the tax — it does not eliminate it. This model deducts the deferred liability rather than pretending the deferral is free, and §1031 still wins on a risk-adjusted basis.

How the sale tax is built

  1. Gain = net sale price − adjusted basis (original basis less accumulated depreciation).
  2. Unrecaptured IRC §1250 gain — the depreciation taken — is taxed at a maximum 25% federal rate. This is the most commonly underestimated line.
  3. Remaining long-term capital gain is taxed at 20% federal plus the 3.8% net investment income tax where applicable.
  4. California taxes the whole gain at ordinary rates with no preferential rate and no out-of-state deferral mechanism without an undertaking.

The §1031 exchange: rules that are not negotiable

45 days

Identify replacement property
In writing, signed, delivered to a person involved in the exchange — notice to your attorney or accountant is not sufficient.

180 days

Complete the exchange
Or the due date of the return for the year of sale, whichever is earlier. Not extendable except in a presidentially declared disaster.

Qualified intermediary

Before closing
You cannot act as your own facilitator, and neither can your agent or anyone who worked for you in that capacity in the previous two years. Taking control of proceeds can disqualify the entire transaction.

Deferral ≠ elimination

Liability carries forward
The deferred gain reduces the basis of the replacement property. This model deducts the deferred liability from the §1031 strategy's economic net worth for exactly that reason.

Two California items that must be resolved before an exchange is structured — both flagged, neither verified here:

1. Out-of-state replacement. California does not defer California-source gain when replacement property sits outside California without an undertaking to file a California return when the replacement is later sold (FTB Form 3840). Get an opinion before identifying replacement property if the plan is to leave the state.

2. Local transfer taxes. LA city and county documentary transfer taxes, and the Measure ULA surcharge on higher-value transfers, were not verified in this session. They affect net proceeds and, at the margin, the timing decision. Confirm with the LA County Registrar-Recorder and the City before pricing a sale.

Wealth preservation: the portfolio view

The brief asked for the best decision "for the property and myself as an individual for the preservation of wealth and opportunity for growth, maintaining a balance that outlines risk factors and maintains a manageable level of risk." Four portfolio observations follow from the data, not from the property:

  • Concentration is the dominant risk, not the cap rate. One parcel, one tenant, one brand, one metro, one catastrophe zone. The model's worst-decile outcome for holding (1.88× NOI) is a concentration outcome.
  • Deferral compounds only if the replacement is better. A §1031 into a single similar asset reproduces the same concentration with a new address. Spread proceeds across at least two assets and, ideally, two metros.
  • Basis and entity structure are unsolved here. Step-up at death, trust ownership, and installment-sale mechanics can each dominate the arithmetic above, and each depends on facts this repository deliberately does not hold. That is a job for counsel, with the numbers in this dossier as inputs.
  • Liquidity has value that NPV does not capture. A sub-$5 million single-tenant asset in a thin, rising-rate market can take 6–12 months to sell. Planning the exit now buys the option to be patient.
