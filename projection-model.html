The scenario model

All money amounts are per $100 of the property's CURRENT yearly net rent, because the actual rent is unknown. Multiply by (actual yearly rent / 100) to convert. The model follows six strategies from 2026 to 2040 for each possible expiry year, in 20,000 seeded trials per year (seed 20261112). It adds yield movements, tenant failure and regional disasters to a closed-form calculation, so that the spread of outcomes is visible, not only the average. Every setting is listed below with its origin. It is a way of thinking with numbers, not a forecast.
