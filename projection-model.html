Projection model

A reproducible 2026–2040 model, published inputs, and the breakevens rather than a guess at value

Deterministic Python. No randomness. Every published number can be regenerated with one command, and every input is either sourced or labelled as an assumption.

Reproduce it: python3 tools/model.py writes data/model_output.json and research/model-report.md. The code is in tools/model.py and is short enough to read in full.

Method

  1. Three paths are modeled. (A) sell now and redeploy the net proceeds; (B) hold through the lease and sell at expiry; (C) renew at a reset rent and hold to 2040. A fourth state, tenant vacates (downtime, $300k of leasing costs, re-let at market), is blended into (C) by a renewal probability.
  2. Path A nets out the real cost stack: 3.00% brokerage, 0.50% closing, 0.11% county transfer tax, 0.45% city transfer tax, and the tiered Measure ULA surtax where the price crosses the official thresholds. S7 ↗ S8 ↗
  3. Proceeds are redeployed at two different rates so the answer cannot hide inside one assumption: the verified 10-year Treasury (4.90%) and the verified net-lease acquisition benchmark (7.3%). S11 ↗ S10 ↗
  4. Everything is compounded to 2040 in nominal dollars, and discounted at 8% for the NPV view. Pre-tax throughout; the tax layer needs the owner's basis.
  5. Breakevens are solved numerically rather than asserted — that is what turns a forecast into a decision rule.

Limits

  • Rent, expiry, basis and debt are placeholders. This is a framework, not an appraisal.
  • No income tax, depreciation recapture, NIIT or California tax is computed - that needs the owner's basis (P05).
  • No wildfire or earthquake loss is simulated. Those sit in the risk register as tail events with insurance as the mitigation.
  • No non-official data is used. The cap rate is an assumption because no government body publishes asset-level cap rates (M51).

Internal decision support — not an appraisal, not tax advice, not investment advice. Every data value on this site links to the official source it was read from on 2026-11-06. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. No personal or parcel-identifying information is published.
