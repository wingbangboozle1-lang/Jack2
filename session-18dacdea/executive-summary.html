Executive Summary: Optimal Decision for the Central LA Jack in the Box NNN Property

A complete synthesis of property/lease mechanics, Central Los Angeles macro-demographics, tenant SEC filings, Business-As-Usual (BAU) & Black Swan risks, and risk-adjusted wealth projections.

Core Verdict: For an individual owner seeking the optimal balance between preservation of wealth and opportunity for growth at a manageable level of risk, the superior course of action is a Two-Stage "Blend-and-Extend Inquiry → Pre-Cliff IRC §1031 Exchange Disposition" (Strategy S2, with immediate fallback to Strategy S1). 1. Why the Decision Is Driven by Lease Term Structure and Tail Risk

The WALT Financing Cliff (<6 Years)

Institutional net-lease portfolios maintain a 10.2-year weighted average lease term (WALT) (S13: NNN REIT, Inc. ↗). When a lease expiring in 2030–2035 drops below 6.0 years of remaining term, commercial lenders restrict 10-year amortizing debt. The buyer pool narrows from passive 1031 investors (who pay ~6.40% cap rates) to value-add buyers (who demand ~7.45%–8.35% cap rates), eroding 18%–24% of property value.

The Tenant Credit & Repricing Reality

Jack in the Box Inc. (JACK) explicitly states in its FY2025 Form 10-K that it renews expiring leases “at then-current market rates” (S11: Jack in the Box Inc. ↗). Meanwhile, comparable sales fell -4.2% in FY2025 and -4.2% YTD Q3 FY2026 (S12: Jack in the Box Inc. ↗), company restaurant margins compressed to 19.6% under California's $20.00/hr AB 1228 wage floor (S09: California Department of I ↗), and management is executing 86 FY25 + 50–100 FY26 closures under JACK on Track.

Unhedged Single-Parcel Catastrophe Risk

Holding a single Central LA parcel to 2040 concentrates 100% of equity in a basin with a 60% 30-year probability of an M≥6.7 earthquake (S22: U.S. Geological Survey ↗), a $1.0 billion California FAIR Plan emergency insurance assessment (S23: California Department of I ↗) following the Jan 2025 Eaton/Palisades fires (16,251–16,264 structures destroyed, S21: County of Los Angeles & CA ↗), and Proposition 13 reassessment friction (S17: Los Angeles County Office ↗).
