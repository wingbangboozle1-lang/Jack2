2. Analytical Takeaways for Property Value & Tenant Viability

Why Central LA Land Value Is Resilient Despite County Out-Migration

While California Department of Finance P-2A projections model Los Angeles County's population easing from 9,853,435 (2025) to 9,727,305 (2030), 9,708,647 (2035), and 9,637,524 (2040) (S04: California Department of F ↗), the urban core of the City of Los Angeles retained population 240 bps better than the county (-0.8% vs -3.2% from 2020 to 2025, S03: U.S. Census Bureau ↗). With 8,304.2 residents/sq mi, 64.0% renter households, $921,200 median home values, and strict discretionary Conditional Use Permit barriers against new drive-thrus (S20: Los Angeles Department of ↗), existing Central LA drive-thru pads possess durable covered-land value.

Why Store-Level Operating Margins Face Structural Compression

The November 4, 2026 BLS release shows local wages rising +3.9% YoY (ECI) and consumer energy prices up +20.3% YoY (S01: U.S. Bureau of Labor Stati ↗), on top of California's statutory $20.00/hour fast-food wage floor (S09: California Department of I ↗) and the City of LA's $18.42/hour minimum wage (S10: City of Los Angeles, Offic ↗). Meanwhile, LA City's 16.5% poverty rate (S03: U.S. Census Bureau ↗) limits how far Jack in the Box can push menu prices without losing traffic—explaining the brand's -4.2% YTD same-store sales (S12: Jack in the Box Inc. ↗) and making lease renewal a market-rate repricing event rather than a rubber stamp.
