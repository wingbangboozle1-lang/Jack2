The tax layer

Not modeled — and that is deliberate. Federal long-term capital gains, unrecaptured Section 1250 depreciation recapture, net investment income tax, and California income tax each depend on the owner's basis, holding period, depreciation history, entity structure, and other income. None of those are in the brief (P05). Publishing a rate stack without them would be a fabrication.

Verified mechanism: Section 1031 permits deferral of gain on the exchange of real property held for productive use or investment, with carryover basis and qualified-intermediary mechanics (S16). S16 ↗

A 1031 exchange is the main tool that separates 'sell' from 'lose 30%+ of the gain'. It is why the recommendation is structured as a sequencing decision, not a binary sell/hold.
