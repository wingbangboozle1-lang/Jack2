Overview

A single-tenant Jack in the Box restaurant in central Los Angeles is leased on net terms, and the lease expires at some point between 2030 and 2035. Should the owner sell before the lease expires, or renew it? This site sets out what 50 official sources say, what remains unknown, and how the choices compare.

Short answer. Ask the tenant for an early extension first, then decide. A refusal points to selling before expiry; an agreement leaves a real choice between keeping a secured income and selling a more valuable lease.

What to do next

  1. Read the lease: expiry date, rent, options and notice dates, who the tenant is, and what happens after a disaster.
  2. Ask the tenant in writing for an early extension. The answer is the most useful single fact available.
  3. Then choose with the decision rule: a refusal points to selling before expiry; an agreement leaves a choice between keeping and selling a more valuable lease.
