The demographic fact that matters most. Los Angeles County has 3.2% fewer residents than the 2020 census base S01 ↗, and the city 0.8% fewer. A quick-service restaurant's volume is a function of the number of people within a short drive. That headwind is slow, but it is the reason the model does not assume the area's rents compound indefinitely at the top of their historical range.

Schools

Los Angeles Unified enrolls 497,105 students in 2025–26, of whom 81,328 (16.4%) are English learners S17 ↗. For a property decision, schools matter through two channels: they anchor families (and therefore daytime and after-school traffic) and they anchor the labour pool. Both are stable. The district's graduation rate is left blank here rather than quoted from a secondary source, because the official figure sits in the California School Dashboard and DataQuest systems, which were not queried (flagged U-13).
