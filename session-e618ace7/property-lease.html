The privacy rule applied to this build. The Owner's identity, the street address, the parcel number, the rent, the loan and the tax basis are not stored in this repository, not in the JSON, not in the HTML and not in the model. The analysis is expressed per $100,000 of annual NOI so that it can be used without ever holding the sensitive figures. A verify script fails the build if an email, phone number, parcel number or street-address pattern appears in any generated page.

Lease clauses to audit before any decision

#ClauseWhy it changes the answer
1Expiration date and commencement of any option periodSets the clock. A 4-year answer is not the same as a 9-year answer.
2Fixed escalators vs CPI with collar, floor and capDetermines whether NOI tracks the verified +3.7% local CPI S03 ↗ or lags it.
3Renewal options held by the tenantOptions transfer bargaining power to the tenant and reduce what a buyer will pay.
4Assignment and sublettingAffects the pool of replacement tenants and therefore the vacancy risk in K01.
5Guarantor and entity structureCorporate guaranty vs franchisee changes the credit entirely.
6Tax, insurance and CAM reimbursement mechanicsA sale resets the Proposition 13 base S12 ↗ and the tenant pays the difference. Renewal pricing depends on it.
7Co-tenancy, kick-out and termination rightsCan void the term you are being asked to pay for.
8Restoration and casualty provisionsDecides who bears the risk in the black-swan scenarios.
9Right of first refusal to purchaseCan eliminate the competitive sale process entirely.
10Holdover and surrender conditionsSets the cost of the transition if the tenant leaves.

Documents to obtain

  • The lease, all amendments, and any side letters or guaranties.
  • Three years of rent roll, CAM reconciliations and the current tax bill.
  • Insurance declarations page and loss runs.
  • Original closing statement, depreciation schedules and current loan statement.
  • A broker opinion of value and three recent comparable sales within two miles.
  • Parcel-level fire hazard zone classification and FEMA flood zone.

Each of these maps to a numbered item on Flagged for review.
