Projection model

A transparent two-equation engine, 20 labelled assumptions, and a seeded 10,000-trial simulation. It is deliberately simple so that it can be argued with. Every assumption is listed with the range tested; every result is per $100,000 of annual NOI.

Read this first. Nothing on this page is a verified fact. The verified facts live on the area, tenant and capital markets pages. This page is the analyst's arithmetic laid on top of them.

The two equations

value = NOI / cap

cap(term, treasury) = 5.25%                       (base cap at 10 years of term)
                    + 0.50 x (10-year Treasury - 4.86%)
                    + 10.0 bp x max(0, 10 - remaining term)
                    floor 4.50%

net sale proceeds   = price - (2.50% brokerage + 0.11% county + 0.45% city + ULA if applicable)
                          - 37.0% x (price - basis)
taxable basis       = 45% of the 2026 value  (assumption A11)

The Measure ULA cliff

At the base cap rate of 5.65%, the property reaches the $5,400,000 Measure ULA threshold at about $305,100 of annual NOI. Below it the 4% City surtax does not apply; above it the surtax applies to the whole price. The thresholds are indexed to chained CPI every year, so the dollar figure moves; the cliff does not go away.

What this model does not do

  • It does not know the rent, the expiry date or the basis. It normalises and assumes.
  • It does not model a leveraged structure, depreciation recapture in detail, or estate planning.
  • It does not model the 1031 execution risk beyond pricing the deferral: a failed exchange is a binary loss, not a distribution.
  • It does not price the property. It prices strategies relative to each other.
