Read both halves of this. Sessions A–E in this repository all correctly quote the fiscal 2025 loss, and several stop there. The same official dataset shows the tenant back in profit in fiscal 2026: +$27.9 million for the nine months to 2026-07-05, against a $86.5 million loss for the same period a year earlier, with the equity deficit narrowing from $938.3 million to $901.3 million. That is an improving credit, not a deteriorating one — and it is still a tenant with negative book equity. Both facts belong in the decision.

What the numbers mean for a landlord

  • Revenue fell 6.7% in fiscal 2025 ($1,571.3m to $1,465.3m) S08 ↗, but fiscal 2026 revenue is not comparable: the Del Taco chain was divested during the year, so the 24% decline in nine-month revenue is a scope change, not a same-store collapse. Do not read it as a demand signal.
  • Negative equity of $901 million S07 ↗ is the structural fact. A company with negative book equity cannot easily absorb a rent increase negotiated against it, and its leases are priced accordingly by buyers.
  • Profitability is thin and recently restored. A landlord should assume the tenant will be a hard negotiator at renewal, not a passive one.
  • The California cost base is specific to this tenant. The $20.00/hour fast-food minimum wage S16 ↗ applies to the brand and its franchisees. That cost sits between the store's sales and the rent.

Questions the Owner should put to the tenant before any extension

  1. Is the lease with the corporate parent or a franchisee, and is there a guaranty? (Not knowable from public sources.)
  2. What is the store's four-wall coverage ratio, and what were its last eight quarters of sales? A renewal decision should be underwritten on the unit, not the brand.
  3. Does the lease contain co-tenancy, kick-out or early-termination rights that would defeat the extension?

Method note

XBRL values are the figures the company itself tagged in its filings, retrieved from the Commission's API with the accession number of the filing that reported them. FY2025 values come from the annual report filed 2025-11-19 (accession 0000807882-25-000072); the nine-month figures come from the quarterly report filed 2026-08-12 (accession 0000807882-26-000091), which is the most recent filing on EDGAR as of 2026-11-07. The fiscal 2026 annual report, covering the year ended 2026-09-27, was not on file.
