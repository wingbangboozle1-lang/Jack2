Decision framework

Six mutually exclusive strategies, scored on the same 10,000 simulated futures. The ranking does not depend on a forecast of Central Los Angeles rents; it depends on the fact that lease term can only fall.

The two leaders differ by less than one percent on risk-adjusted wealth, and the choice between them is a choice about control: extending first keeps the asset and creates value but exposes the Owner to three to six more years of tenant and hazard risk; selling now is certain but gives up the extension premium. That is why the recommendation is a sequence rather than a single transaction: extend, then sell — which is the same economic act as a 1031 sale, just executed later and with more term to sell.

Walk-away terms

  • Never accept an extension shorter than ten years, or one that reduces the rent.
  • Never accept a kick-out, co-tenancy or early-termination clause in exchange for term you are being told is valuable.
  • Never let the 1031 identification window be the reason a closing slips: identify replacement property before closing.
  • Never treat an unsigned term sheet as an extension. Documented, signed, and recorded matters.
