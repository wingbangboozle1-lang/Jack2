Executive summary

One page for a reader with ten minutes. Built from 396 values read at 50 official sources on 2026-11-12; 44 further values are blank because they could not be confirmed or are private.

Ask the tenant for an early extension first, then decide. A refusal points to selling before expiry; an agreement leaves a real choice between keeping a secured income and selling a more valuable lease.

  • Asking costs little and commits the owner to nothing.
  • A signed extension raises what a buyer would pay and protects a holder against a change of mind at the tenant.
  • A refusal is information: it is the strongest available signal that the tenant may leave, received while there is still lease term to sell.
