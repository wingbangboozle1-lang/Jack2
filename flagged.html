5. Assumptions that are not data

The model needs numbers the brief does not supply. They are listed in full on Valuation inputs with their confidence level. The most consequential: benchmark rent ($150,000/yr), lease expiry used as 2032, exit cap 6.50%, and a 75% renewal probability. Change any of them in data/valuation.json and re-run python3 tools/model.py.

6. Close-out checklist

  1. Pull the APN and run the four parcel-level lookups: FEMA NFHL, CAL FIRE/LACoFD FHSZ, CGS EQ Zapp, and an insurance quote.
  2. Obtain the executed lease and confirm the option notice deadline and the option-rent mechanism.
  3. Obtain a broker opinion of value and an appraisal; replace assumption A5 with the actual implied cap rate.
  4. Obtain the adjusted basis and depreciation schedule from the CPA; then, and only then, model the tax and the 1031.
  5. Re-run the model with real inputs and compare against the decision triggers on the Decision page.
