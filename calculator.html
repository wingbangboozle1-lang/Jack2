How the two paths are computed

  • Sell now: transfer tax is the city tier for the sale price plus the county rate, applied to the whole price (tiers TX-006 to TX-008; county TX-013). The seller's share is deducted with selling costs to give the amount realised. The taxable gain is that amount less the adjusted basis. Depreciation recapture is taxed at the recapture rate, up to the depreciation taken; the rest of the gain at the long-term rate. The 3.8% tax and the California rate are applied to the gain (TX-043, TX-060).
  • Hold and renew: the combined rate on the rent is applied to taxable rent. The after-tax rent is discounted over the years entered (end-of-year payments), and the after-tax cash at the end is discounted too. The result is the present value of the hold path.
  • Difference: after-tax sale cash minus the hold path's present value, in today's dollars.
