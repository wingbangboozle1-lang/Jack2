After-tax calculator

A browser calculator that sets the after-tax cash from a sale now against the present value of a negotiated renewal. Every box starts blank, nothing is stored or sent, and no winner is shown until every input is entered and checked.

Private to you. Nothing you type is sent, saved or shared. This page makes no network call, stores nothing, and forgets everything when you close it. Keep your figures in the private file and type only what you need.

Every box starts blank. No figure is filled in for you, except the buttons that copy a verified rate from the ledger.

How the arithmetic works

StepWhat it does
GainSale price, less sale costs, less adjusted tax basis.
Depreciation recaptureThe part of a gain up to the depreciation taken, taxed at the recapture rate. The rest of the gain is taxed at the federal rate you enter.
Investment income taxIf you answer yes, the investment income tax rate is applied to the whole gain or rent. Its rate is a ledger value.
CaliforniaThe California rate you enter is applied to the whole gain, because California taxes gains at ordinary rates.
RenewalEach year's rent is grown by the increase you enter, taxed at the rates you enter, and discounted to today at the discount rate.
End of renewed termThe end-of-term sale is taxed with the same rates as the sale now. This is an assumption, stated here.
Left outDepreciation deductions during the hold, the limit on deducting capital losses, and any state rule the page does not name. Those are for the private model.

Rates and thresholds come from the ledger rows named beside each box, each linked to its official source: Taxes and costs, and Capital markets.
