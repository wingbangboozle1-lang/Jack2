Overview

Anonymized, source-linked decision dashboard for a central Los Angeles Jack in the Box NNN investment with a lease expiring sometime in 2030–2035. Working recommendation: run a dual track now — obtain executable sale pricing and a written extension proposal in parallel. Do not let the lease drift into a short-term rollover without a dated decision gate. A definitive sell/renew/hold ranking is not supportable until rent, exact expiry, tenant entity, debt, tax basis and offers are known.
