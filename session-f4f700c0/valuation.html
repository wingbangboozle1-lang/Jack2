Method and limits

  • Annual cash flows 2027–2036 are reinvested at the scenario rate; terminal value uses next-year NOI divided by the scenario exit cap, net of sale costs.
  • Renewal and vacancy are separate deterministic branches, not probability-weighted expected values.
  • ULA cannot be applied to normalized NOI units without a dollar scale and jurisdiction; use the tax page helper and actual transaction facts.
  • Area projections inform context only; they do not mechanically set rent, cap rates or terminal value.
