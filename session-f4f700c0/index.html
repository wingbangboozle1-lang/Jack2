Executive summary

  • Preserve optionality. The lease expires sometime in 2030–2035. Start price discovery and extension discussions before the remaining term becomes the dominant pricing factor.
  • Do not use brand averages as property rent. JACK franchise AUV and corporate financials are context, not the subject operator's sales, guaranty or rent coverage.
  • Area fundamentals are mixed, not a rent forecast. DOF projects county population decline but household growth; SCAG's adopted planning forecast is more growth-oriented. Employment is shifting toward education/health while information contracts.
  • Tenant credit requires entity-level work. The current franchisor filing reports negative book equity, lower operating cash flow and franchisee payment delays/deferrals, alongside positive net earnings and stated liquidity sufficiency.
  • Taxes and hazards can dominate net outcomes. City ULA thresholds, recapture, NIIT, 1031 timing, insurance, earthquake/flood/fire exposure and environmental status must be verified at the parcel/owner level.
