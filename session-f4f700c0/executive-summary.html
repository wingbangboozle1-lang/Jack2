Executive summary & decision framework

Conditional recommendation, strategy gates and normalized scenario snapshot.

Best course under current information: dual-track price discovery and extension inquiry, then choose using executable net proceeds and risk capacity. This is a conditional process recommendation, not a fabricated property ranking.

Decision gates

  1. Document gate: obtain lease/options, rent roll, tenant entity/guaranty, store sales/payment history, debt, tax basis, insurance and property condition.
  2. Market gate: obtain at least one executable sale offer and one written extension proposal; verify jurisdiction and closing-date tax treatment.
  3. Economics gate: compare after-tax, after-debt, after-cost proceeds and replacement-asset quality. Use the normalized model only as a sensitivity framework.
  4. Risk gate: confirm the owner can absorb a long vacancy, insurance shock or correlated regional disaster without a forced sale.
  5. Timing gate: set a dated review before the lease enters the short-term window implied by the actual expiry; do not wait passively until 2030–2035.
