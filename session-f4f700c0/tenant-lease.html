Lease diligence checklist

  • Exact expiry, options, notice deadlines, assignment/change-of-control clauses and guaranty burn-off.
  • Base rent, escalations, percentage rent, audits, late fees, payment history and store-level rent coverage.
  • Roof/structure, parking, drive-thru rights, grease interceptor, ADA, environmental and capital-replacement duties.
  • Insurance requirements, casualty/condemnation, abatement, co-tenancy/access and landlord default remedies.
