Exchange and sale checklist

  • Confirm city vs county jurisdiction, closing date, exemptions and county transfer tax separately.
  • Compute adjusted basis, depreciation recapture, capital gain, NIIT and California treatment with qualified tax professionals.
  • For 1031: real-property replacement, qualified intermediary, 45-day identification, 180-day-or-return-due-date receipt, boot/debt replacement and FTB 3840 if California-source gain is exchanged out of state.
  • Ordinary REIT shares are generally not direct like-kind replacement property; structure-specific exceptions require counsel.
