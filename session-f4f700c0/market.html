Interpretation

  • DOF population decline with household growth implies smaller average households, not necessarily weaker convenience/food demand.
  • SCAG's higher planning path reflects policy, land-use and migration assumptions; it is not a competing observation to average with DOF.
  • Income, rent burden and housing costs matter for value-oriented QSR demand, but city medians cannot identify the subject block's customer base.
