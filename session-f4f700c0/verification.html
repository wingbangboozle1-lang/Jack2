What was checked

  • Official-source ledger: every evidence row has a primary official link; unknown observed values are blank.
  • Line-level repository inventory: file hashes, line counts, JSON/Python/HTML structure and identifier patterns.
  • Targeted source-value audit: Census, BLS/EDD, SEC, DOF/SCAG, CDE, Treasury, tax and hazard sources were independently retrieved where available.
  • Model arithmetic: normalized cash-flow, terminal-value, ULA boundary and breakeven tests.
  • Site integrity: internal links, source links, privacy scan and static build checks.

Limits

  • This is not a certification that every historical narrative sentence is true; legacy generated pages are preserved and flagged where assumption-driven.
  • External endpoints can fail or move; unavailable items are left blank and flagged rather than replaced with third-party numbers.
  • No private owner/operator documents were supplied or persisted. The public product contains placeholders and blanks only.
