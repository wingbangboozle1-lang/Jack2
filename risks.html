How the black-swan scenarios were calibrated

The January 2025 Eaton and Palisades fires are the only recent, officially quantified metropolitan-scale loss event for this county, so they set the scale for the tail scenarios:

The honest conclusion about tail risk: a single-tenant, single-parcel asset cannot diversify away earthquake, wildfire, or insurance-capacity risk. Insurance transfers the loss but not the downtime, the deductible, or the premium shock. The only structural mitigation is a liquidity reserve sized to the deductible plus 12 months of NOI — and, if the owner's risk tolerance is low, selling into a diversified exchange.
