Los Angeles Labor Market & Sector Divergence (Sep 2026)

Corrected directly against the Nov 4, 2026 BLS release and Oct 16, 2026 CA EDD report: LA County seasonally adjusted unemployment improved to 4.8% (down from 5.6% in Sep 2025; NSA 4.5%, Metro 4.4%). Growth concentrates in Education & Health (+49.1k / +3.8% MSA) and Leisure & Hospitality (+11.5k / +1.5%) while Information (-11.4k / -5.7%) contracts.

Source: BLS Los Angeles Area Economic Summary (Nov 4, 2026) — Sep 2025 to Sep 2026 NSA Nonfarm Payroll Change in Thousands [S01].

Wages & Inflation Pressures

  • AB 1228 Fast Food Floor: $20.00/hr (60+ national units) vs. $18.42/hr City of LA general minimum wage (eff. 7/1/2026) and $16.90/hr CA statewide [S19] [S20].
  • LA Metro Sep 2026 CPI-U: +3.7% YoY (Food +2.8%, Energy +20.3%), while LA CSA Employment Cost Index rose +3.9% YoY vs. 3.1% US [S01].

Regional Catalysts Ahead of LA28

  • Metro D Line Subway Section 1 Opened May 8, 2026: Wilshire/La Brea, Fairfax, and La Cienega stations now live; Sections 2–3 follow in 2027 ahead of the 2028 Olympics [S07].
  • Port of Los Angeles All-Time Record: 1,042,652 TEUs in Sep 2026 (9M YTD 8,081,625 TEUs, +3.38% YoY) sustaining regional trade & logistics demand [S08].
