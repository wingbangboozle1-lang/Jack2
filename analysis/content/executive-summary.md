# Executive summary

**The question.** OWNER-01 holds a single-tenant, triple-net Jack in the Box in Central Los Angeles. The lease expires somewhere between 2030 and 2035. Should the property be sold before expiry, or held and renewed?

**The answer.** Sell. Market the property now and close within 12–18 months, using a §1031 exchange to move the capital into a diversified net-lease position. Do not simply wait for the lease to expire.

This is a ranking conclusion, not a price opinion. The property's rent, NOI, basis, size, address and lease terms were never supplied and are deliberately not reconstructed — publishing them would breach the privacy requirement and inventing them would breach the accuracy requirement. Everything below is therefore expressed **per $1.00 of current annual NOI**, so the owner can multiply by their own NOI once and read the answer in dollars.

## Why selling wins

1. **A structural yield gap sits in front of the owner.** A single-tenant LA quick-service asset with a few years of term is priced, in this model, at a 6.00% going-in cap. A diversified net-lease portfolio was acquiring property in 2025 at a 7.3% initial cash yield (Realty Income, SEC filing). Exchanging out of a 6.0% asset into a 7.3% one raises income on day one and spreads the risk across hundreds of tenants. The trade-off is lower growth — 1.3% same-store versus a 2.5% contractual escalator — and the model says the yield still wins.

2. **The tenant's side of the table is deteriorating.** 93.0% of the Jack in the Box system is franchised, so the tenant of record is most likely a single-operator small business rather than the corporate parent. The franchisor reported same-store sales of (4.2%) for FY2025, closed 86 units, and guided to 50–100 more closures in FY2026. Franchise bad-debt expense rose. The model puts the cumulative probability of tenant default before expiry at **18.8% if the lease ends in 2030 and 39.9% if it ends in 2035**.

3. **The owner does not control the renewal.** "Renew the lease" is not a decision the owner can make unilaterally; renewal options in QSR leases sit with the tenant. The real choice is sell, hold, or pay an inducement to extend. Holding is a bet on someone else's boardroom.

4. **Rates have turned against values.** The 10-year Treasury was 4.84% on 5 November 2026, up 65 bp since January, with the 30-year at 5.25% and the FOMC raising its target range to 3.75–4.00% in September. A 6.00% cap is only a 116 bp spread over the 10-year. Cap rates have room to widen, and each +50 bp removes roughly 7.7% of value.

5. **The downside is asymmetric.** The model's 10th-percentile outcome for sell-now is 29.49 per $1 of NOI against a starting value of about 16.67 — it never loses money in 20,000 paths. Holding to expiry has a 10th-percentile outcome of about 21.8–22.6 and a median compound return of 4.5–5.0%, versus 7.1% for selling.

## The numbers

| Strategy | Median wealth per $1 NOI (2036) | 10th percentile | Median CAGR | Wins vs hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sell now, §1031 into diversified net lease | 33.26 | 29.49 | 7.1% | 98.2% |
| Sell in 2027–2028 | ≈ same as sell-now | ≈ 29.49 | 7.1% | ≈ 98% |
| Extend, then sell | 28.31 | 22.23 | 5.4% | 77.1% |
| Hold to expiry | 26.70 | 22.19 | 4.8% | — |

Break-even: holding only beats selling if the 2036 exit cap is below roughly **4.7%**. That is below any plausible current LA net-lease cap and would require a collapse in long-term rates. The conclusion is therefore robust to reasonable changes in the exit cap — but *not* to a change in the owner's objective.

## The two reasons to overrule this

- **Estate planning.** If the owner intends to hold until death, the federal basis step-up can outweigh every calculation above. That is a personal and legal determination, not a market one, and it is flagged rather than modelled.
- **Redevelopment.** The site's land value may exceed its income value. Section 1 of the Metro D Line extension opened in May 2026 and Sections 2 and 3 are under construction along the Wilshire corridor; central-LA transit accessibility is improving. If the parcel can be rezoned or assembled, the option value may dominate.
