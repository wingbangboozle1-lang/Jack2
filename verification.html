Source tiers

Tier A is a government or regulator record. Tier B is an SEC filing by the tenant. Tier C is an issuer investor-relations disclosure, used only where no regulator publishes the figure (net-lease acquisition yields). Tier D — industry commentary, broker blogs, data aggregators — is not used for any value in this workbook.

What was deliberately not verified

  • Asset-level cap rate. No government body publishes it. A broker opinion of value is required (F02).
  • Parcel hazard status. Needs the APN: FEMA panel, CAL FIRE FHSZ, CGS liquefaction (M82–M84).
  • Insurance pricing. Needs a broker quote; no official rate exists (M85).
  • Tax on sale. Needs the owner's basis and depreciation history (P05).
  • Los Angeles city minimum wage. DIR states local rates may be higher but the city rate was not confirmed officially here (M63).
