Scenario design

Projection framework—not a fabricated forecast

No property-specific forecast is publishable yet. The asset's NOI, sale value, tax basis, lease dates, hazard, insurance and investor-specific constraints are missing. We do not fill them with generic cap rates, location guesses, or placeholder returns.

Once the required inputs are verified, build a reproducible annual after-tax cash-flow model from the present through at least the confirmed lease expiry and a common post-expiry horizon. Every input must carry source, date, geography, unit, confidence/status and whether observed or assumed.

Model architecture

  1. Use lease-derived rent and verified owner expense/capital obligations; independently reconcile cash receipts and tax/assessor records.
  2. Use sale comparables only after matching asset type, lease term, rent, tenant credit, location and date. Report a range; market cap rates are transaction evidence, not official statistics.
  3. Separate operating cash flow, land/building residual value, sale friction, debt repayment, taxes, and reinvestment. Tax and Section 1031 outcomes require individualized professional advice.
  4. Scenarios: bear/base/upside for macro, local demand, rent growth, exit yield, expenses, rates and liquidity; severe but plausible shocks for tenant failure, earthquake, wildfire/smoke, flood/utility disruption and insurance nonrenewal.
  5. Use sensitivity/tornado analysis and break-even conditions first. Monte Carlo is optional only where distributions are evidence-based; do not call an assumed probability a forecast.
  6. Report median and downside percentiles, probability of shortfall against a privately selected wealth/liquidity floor, and model limitations. Never report false precision.

Public signal currently available

Official Census and BLS observations appear on Area Evidence. They support context only: they do not predict site sales, Jack in the Box revenue, property rent, cap rates, or value. School, zoning, trade-area and parcel hazards remain open questions until location is verified.
