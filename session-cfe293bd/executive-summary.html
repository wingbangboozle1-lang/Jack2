Make no commitment until after-tax net values and downside risks are comparable.

Best decision framework

Evaluate sell now / sell later, extension then hold, extension then sale, and hold to expiry under common assumptions. Compare after-tax cash flows, sale friction, lease income and residual value, tenant-default/vacancy, capital needs, insurance, liquidity, concentration and reinvestment execution. Optimize for the owner's stated goals—wealth preservation, growth, and manageable risk—not a single headline return.

A renewal should be selected only if the documented extension terms and downside-adjusted hold value beat credible net sale-and-reinvestment alternatives while satisfying liquidity and concentration limits. A sale should be selected only if defensible net bids beat the hold/extension alternative and the post-sale plan is executable. No numeric hurdle is set because required private inputs and risk preferences are unknown.

Suggested sequence

  1. Privately assemble the executed lease, amendments, option/notice calendar, title/parcel, rent ledger, expense history, tax basis, debt/payoff, insurance and inspection materials.
  2. Have counsel/CPA verify legal and tax issues; keep personal information in private professional records only.
  3. Obtain written renewal terms and independent sale valuations/offers in parallel; test comparable lease term, rent, tenant credit and property condition.
  4. Complete parcel-specific demand, land-use, school-boundary, hazard and insurance review only after location is privately verified.
  5. Run bear/base/upside and severe-event cases; document assumptions, confidence ranges, break-even points and chosen risk limits.
  6. Revisit each quarter and before each confirmed lease notice milestone; update official data vintages and market evidence.

What could alter the recommendation?

A very attractive firm extension, poor net sale bids, an adverse tax/transaction result, significant uninsured loss exposure, weak tenant credit, a major physical defect, local redevelopment constraints, or strong alternative use/value could each change the best path.
