Why the geography matters

  • Population, income, poverty, education: Census QuickFacts observations are city/county-wide and hide neighborhood variation. Use tract/block-group data or a defensible trade area only after the parcel is privately verified; include ACS margins of error and vintages.
  • Jobs: BLS metro data aggregate a large multi-city area. The published +0.5% total payroll job change for September 2025–September 2026 is explicitly not statistically significant at 90% confidence; the latest estimate is preliminary.
  • Schools: citywide education attainment is not school system quality. First verify attendance boundaries, then use current school-specific California Dashboard outcomes and enrollment—not a guessed school.
  • Geography, access and economic activity: trade-area population, traffic counts, daytime workers, competition, planned development and retailer/store performance remain unverified. Do not convert regional indicators into a property value or rent forecast.
