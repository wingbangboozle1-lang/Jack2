Privacy safeguards

Privacy is a hard requirement

Public-site rule: never publish personally identifying, parcel-identifying, tenant-contact, taxpayer, banking, or authentication information.

Not published and not requested here

  • Owner names, personal addresses, emails, phone numbers, signatures or contact details.
  • Property street address, APN, legal description, parcel map, access credentials, or combinations that uniquely identify a private owner/property.
  • Tax returns, basis, debt/account numbers, bank information, insurance policy identifiers, unredacted lease documents, tenant contacts, or personally attributable wealth information.

Use placeholders and minimum necessary data

Use a random non-identifying asset key for private professional workflows. For public reporting, publish only properly aggregated or rounded information after a disclosure review. Keep source documents in access-controlled systems managed by counsel/CPA/advisors, never in GitHub Pages or this repository.

Browser suggestion controls

Suggestion-review selections are stored only in local browser storage. No decision, identity, or private document is submitted to the site. Avoid using public/shared devices for sensitive decision work.

Privacy limitation

Repository history and static hosting can preserve old content. If any personal information is discovered in existing repository material, it must be treated as a release-blocking incident: stop publication, request authorized removal from current and historical copies, and review repository and hosting history. This dashboard itself contains no owner or parcel identifiers.

Internal decision support only. Not an appraisal, legal opinion, tax advice, or investment advice.
