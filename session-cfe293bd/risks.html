Risk balance

Renewal retains tenant credit, lease, physical asset, local-market and catastrophe exposure. Sale removes direct property and tenant exposure but creates transaction/tax friction, reinvestment risk, and possible loss of upside. Waiting preserves options but concentrates risk and can miss contract deadlines. Set tolerances for liquidity, concentration, uninsured loss and expected downside before comparing paths; these personal risk settings should remain private.
