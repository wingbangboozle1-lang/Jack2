Decision gate

  1. Protect the option clock: have counsel confirm actual expiry, options, notice periods, and obligations.
  2. Underwrite the asset: verify rent/NOI, lease allocations, repair condition, tax basis, debt and insurance privately.
  3. Run both paths: obtain a written extension offer and independently supported sale range; calculate net proceeds and risk-adjusted cash flows.
  4. Decide at a documented hurdle: extend only if its risk-adjusted after-tax value exceeds sale/reinvestment and meets liquidity/diversification needs; sell only if net offers clear the same hurdle and a viable reinvestment plan exists.

Until steps 1–3 are complete, neither “sell now” nor “renew” is supported by the verified evidence.
