Methodology

How this dossier was built, and the limits of what it can tell you

The research method, the verification rule, the privacy rule, the projection method, and an explicit list of what this is not.

Session arena-8149683c · 2026-11-06 · reproducible from the repository

1. Research method

  1. Source-first, not question-first. For each question, the official publisher was identified before any number was sought: SEC EDGAR for the tenant and for the cap-rate anchor, BLS for labour, Census for demographics, the Federal Reserve for rates, the LA County Assessor for property tax, Cal/OSHA-adjacent state agencies for labour law, CAL FIRE / USGS / FEMA for hazards, IRS for tax, and SCAG for the regional forecast.
  2. Primary over secondary, always. Where a secondary summary and a primary filing disagreed or where only the secondary existed, the secondary was registered at Tier C and excluded from every calculation.
  3. Numbers were copied, not recalled. Every VERIFIED row quotes a figure that appeared in a document retrieved in this session.
  4. Derived values show their formula. There are 2 of them, and each one states the arithmetic in its note so it can be rechecked by hand.

2. The verification rule

If an official source could not confirm a value, the value is blank. It is not estimated, interpolated, rounded from a related figure, or filled from general knowledge. The row is published with UNVERIFIED so the gap is visible rather than papered over.

Current tally: 126 verified, 2 derived, 2 flagged, 27 blank, out of 157 rows.

3. The privacy rule

No personal information is published. The specific controls applied:

  • No street address and no APN appears anywhere in this repository or on this site. The asset is described only as "central Los Angeles".
  • No owner name, entity name, phone number, email address, account number or tax ID appears in any template, data file or page.
  • No third-party personal data. The executives named in the tenant's 10-K are public officers of a public company disclosing under securities law; they are not reproduced here.
  • Consequence accepted: because the parcel cannot be located, every parcel-level lookup is impossible. That is a real analytical cost, and it is why 14 rows are blank. Privacy was treated as the higher-priority constraint.

4. The projection method

Four decisions, one discounted-cash-flow engine. Net rent plus sale proceeds, discounted at the owner's required return. Branches differ only in exit date, exit cap and whether a lease roll occurs inside the holding period.

The sale valuation uses the identity a net-lease buyer actually uses: the present value at the market cap of contract rent through expiry, plus the present value at the market cap of the reversion re-let at market rent. Pricing a short lease as rent / cap would overstate it badly.

Uncertainty is placed where it actually lives — the exit cap rate, the roll outcome and the black-swan overlay — rather than smeared uniformly across every input. Contract rent is contractual and is taken as given within each scenario.

The Monte Carlo is deterministic under seed 20261106 with an explicit Box-Muller transform, so the checked-in output is byte-reproducible. --check fails loudly on drift.

5. What this is not

Not an appraisal. No licensed appraiser inspected the property. No USPAP standard was followed. Do not use this for lending, financial reporting, or a tax filing.

Not investment advice. It is an internal decision document prepared for one owner about one asset.

Not a tax opinion. §1031 and depreciation recapture are described structurally only. A CPA must confirm every tax statement before it is acted on.

Not a substitute for the documents. The five blocking gaps on the flagged page materially change the answer. Until they are closed, the recommendation is a rule, not a verdict.

Not current beyond 2026-11-06. Rates, cap rates, unemployment and forecasts all move. Every row carries an as_of date precisely so this does not silently rot.

6. Reproducing the build

python3 model/project_model.py — regenerate the projection and Monte Carlo.

python3 model/project_model.py --check — assert the output has not drifted.

python3 model/build_site.py — regenerate every page from the JSON.

python3 verification/verify.py — audit every row, every source reference and every internal link.

No third-party packages are required. Python 3.8 or later.
