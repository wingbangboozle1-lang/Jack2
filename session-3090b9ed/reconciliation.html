Where they agree
  • A passive hold into the final years before expiry is the worst managed outcome - every workbook rejects it.
  • Opening an extension / blend-and-extend conversation now is recommended by every workbook that states a sequence.
  • A Section 1031 sale is the preferred exit vehicle over a taxable sale wherever a replacement pipeline exists.
  • The owner-private facts (rent, expiry, options, basis) are missing everywhere and gate any property-specific number.
Where they disagree
  • The single breakeven number: 6.52% (session-313ba936) vs 6.97% (root, session-d9c94ed0) vs ~6.8% asset-yield framing (session-5f296f9d) vs 7.81% exit-cap / 8.64% reinvest (this session). Different assumption sets, not different data - flagged as IR-09.
  • Whether renew-and-hold can ever win: session-5f296f9d says yes on expected value with strong terms; session-313ba936's ranking and docs/ put renew-and-hold last.

The qualitative decision is robust across fifteen independent analyses: extend first, keep the sale ready, never drift to expiry unmanaged. The quantitative triggers must be re-solved against this owner's actual rent, expiry and appraisal before any irreversible move.
