Projection system

Deterministic scenario grids plus a seeded Monte Carlo (10,000 trials, seed 20261111) over sell-now / hold-to-expiry / extend-then-sell / do-nothing, with business-as-usual and black-swan hazards. All property-specific inputs are illustrative assumptions - the model runs per $1 of current annual NOI so no private rent number is required.
