Q05procurableIs the parcel inside City of Los Angeles limits (Measure ULA applies) or in unincorporated county (no city DTT, no ULA)?(open)High. Q16answeredWhat are the current benchmark interest rates?10-year Treasury 4.88% (observation 2026-11-09); federal funds target upper limit 4.00% (2026-11-10).Sets the reinvestment benchmark every hold-vs-sell comparison must beat.S03, S04
Q17answeredWhat would a sale cost in transfer taxes?County DTT 0.11% ($1.10 per $1,000). If inside City of LA: base 0.45% plus ULA 4.0% (price $5.4M-$10.9M) or 5.5% (price >= $10.9M), i.e. 4.45% / 5.95% city total, on top of the county 0.11%. Thresholds effective for closings after 2026-06-30.A city-location sale carries roughly 4.6%-6.1% of price in transfer taxes alone - a first-order input to sell-now.S05, S06
Q18answeredCan a sale be tax-deferred?Yes, if structured as a Section 1031 like-kind exchange of real property held for business or investment (IRS Form 8824 instructions, 2026).Makes the sell-now branch viable without immediate capital-gains tax - subject to Q11 and Q12.S18 ↗

Q20answeredHow bad could a local natural disaster be?Recent precedent: the January 2025 Eaton and Palisades fires burned 14,021 and 23,448 acres, destroyed 9,414 and 6,837 structures, and caused an estimated $5.2-$10.1 billion of lost economic output. Seismic: USGS gives the Los Angeles region a 60% probability of a M6.7+ earthquake within 30 years (46% for M7.0+, 31% for M7.5+).Black-swan losses are real and documented; insurance (Q08) and parcel hazard class (Q09) determine how much of it lands on this owner.S12, S16
