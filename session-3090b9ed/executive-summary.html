Executive summary

Bottom line: DUAL TRACK, time-boxed: (1) open a confidential extension / blend-and-extend inquiry now targeting >=10 years of term at or above in-place rent with CPI-linked escalators and a strengthened guaranty; (2) in parallel, prepare a Section 1031 sale so the asset can be sold quickly if the extension fails or the appraisal clears the breakeven; (3) never let the asset drift into the final 3-4 years of term unmanaged.

What the data says

Large but shrinking consumer base (city -0.8%, county -3.2% since 2020), re-hiring labour market (metro unemployment 4.4%, Sep 2026), slow growth forecast (real GCP +1.4%/+1.5%), highest county personal income in the U.S. ($818.5B, 2024).

What the tenant says

Jack in the Box Q3 FY2026: system same-store sales -1.1%, 13 net closures in the quarter, count 2,115, refinancing completed. Stabilising but still contracting - extension leverage is moderate, not strong.

What the model says

Extend-then-sell has the highest modelled mean (41.46 per $1 NOI) and wins 56.6% of trials; sell-now has the best tail (P05 27.98, lowest volatility); do-nothing never wins. Dual track keeps both alive.

What is missing

16 open questions, including the four owner-private facts that gate every property-specific number: contract rent, expiry, option terms, basis. Parcel hazard, flood, insurance and in-city status are also unknown.

Transaction costs on a sale

County DTT 0.11% everywhere; if inside City of LA add 0.45% base + 4.0%/5.5% ULA = 4.56%-6.06% all-in (thresholds $5.4M/$10.9M for closings after 2026-06-30).

Black-swan exposure

USGS: 60% chance of M6.7+ in the LA region within 30 years. 2025 fires burned 37,469 acres combined and cost $5.2-10.1B in output. Insurance and parcel hazard class are unverified - confirm before any long hold.
