Reading the labour market. The county added jobs at roughly the national rate while its unemployment rate fell by more than a point. The composition matters more than the total: education and health (+49.1k) and leisure and hospitality (+11.5k) grew, while information (−11.4k), manufacturing (−5.4k) and financial activities (−5.1k) shrank. A QSR in a dense urban trade area is levered to the first two and to lunchtime office traffic that the last three support.

What this means for the property

  • Employment is a support, not a growth story. +0.5% payroll growth (E08) will not lift restaurant volumes much; it does underwrite the rent that is already being paid.
  • CPI of 3.7% (E18) is the ceiling for escalators. A lease that resets at CPI hands the landlord roughly the growth assumed in the base case of this model — which is why the base case uses 3.0% and the bull case uses the verified 3.7%.
  • Wage floors compress the franchisee's margin. $20.00/hour for fast food (W02) and $16.90 statewide (W01) raise the cost base of exactly this format; that pressure, not the landlord's rent, is what drives closure decisions.
  • Energy inflation of 20.3% (E20) is a cost shock to a drive-through. It is also a signal that input volatility in this sector is not over.
