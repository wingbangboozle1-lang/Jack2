The seven publication rules

  1. A "verified" row must have a non-empty value and a source id that resolves to an https URL.
  2. A "blank" row must have an empty value and must appear on the flagged page.
  3. Every source must be cited by at least one row; an orphan source is an error.
  4. No source may be an aggregator, a news article, a broker survey or a language model.
  5. Every internal link must resolve to a file that exists.
  6. No page may contain an e-mail address, a phone number, a street address, an APN, an account number or a person's name.
  7. The published strategy figures must equal the figures in the model output — the site cannot drift from the engine.

Limitations stated plainly

  • This is decision support, not an appraisal, tax advice or investment advice.
  • No rent, address, size, basis, loan or lease document was supplied, so nothing here asserts this parcel's value.
  • Eight earlier sessions reached overlapping but not identical conclusions. This workbook reconciles their evidence, not their recommendations; where they differ, the difference is analytical judgement, and it is visible on the workspaces page.
  • Cap rates, basis, renewal probability and loss given hazard are assumptions. They are published with ranges and tested.
