Anatomy of a triple-net QSR lease

What "NNN" transfers to the tenant

Property taxes, building insurance and common-area maintenance sit with the tenant. That is why a net-lease asset is priced on the credit of the tenant and the term of the lease rather than on the landlord's operating skill.

It does not transfer: the roof, the structure, the parking lot or the landlord's own financing — and in a California net lease it rarely transfers earthquake exposure.

Why the 2030–2035 window is itself a risk

A buyer prices a lease they can read. Five years of uncertainty inside the term is priced as a discount even before any negotiation begins. Fixing the expiry — by extension — is worth more than most rent bumps.

Running the hold case across each expiry year from 2030 to 2035 moves certainty-equivalent wealth by $33,318 per $100,000 of NOI on this model.

Tenant credit

Both halves of the story. The company has lost money and carries negative equity; it has also returned to profit in the current fiscal year. A summary that quotes only one of those is not a credit assessment.
