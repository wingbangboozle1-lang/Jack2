What each source is used for

Demand and demographics

Census Bureau QuickFacts for the city and, separately, the county (S02, S03). The county must be called by its own slug: the combined two-geography URL only works with losangelescountycalifornia, which is the defect recorded as R-01.

Labour and prices

BLS Los Angeles Area Economic Summary, updated 2026-11-04 (S01). It is a PDF, so the values are read from the published tables rather than a database extract.

Capital markets

The Treasury's own daily par yield curve for November 2026 (S04). Every rate on the valuation page is a row in that table, dated.

Tenant credit

SEC EDGAR: the filing index for the FY2025 10-K and Q3 FY2026 10-Q (S05, S06), plus the XBRL company-concept endpoints for net income and equity (S07, S08) so the figures are the filed ones, not a press summary.

Tax

City of Los Angeles Office of Finance for the transfer tax and Measure ULA (S09); the County Assessor for Proposition 13 (S10); the IRS fact sheet for §1031 (S11).

Hazard and schools

USGS for the UCERF3 probabilities (S12); CAL FIRE/OSFM for the fire-hazard framework (S13); CDE for LAUSD (S14); DIR for the wage floors (S15, S16).
