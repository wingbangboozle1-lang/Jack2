Sell before expiry, or renew?

A decision workbook for one property: a single-tenant Jack in the Box on an NNN lease in Central Los Angeles, expiring 2030–2035. Every number is linked to the official page it came from.

Recommendation: do not let the lease run to expiry. Extend now, then sell in 2032 through a §1031 exchange.

The lease expires somewhere in 2030–2035. Every path that ends in a discretionary sale while the tenant is still paying ranks above holding to expiry or renewing and holding. Extending now and selling in 2032 with term left is first on risk-adjusted wealth; selling now through a §1031 exchange is 2.1% behind it and wins outright if rent growth disappoints. A taxable sale costs 4.4% of certainty-equivalent wealth. Holding to expiry has a higher median than selling, but a 5th-percentile outcome $176,615 lower.
