Privacy

Personal information privacy is the top priority of this engagement.

  • No owner name, entity name, street address, parcel number (APN), loan number, tenant contact, or financial account appears in this repository or on the public site.
  • The property is referred to only as “the Subject Property (Central Los Angeles)”. Identifying fields are replaced by placeholders (see Property & Lease).
  • Any personal information is internal use only — never on the website or any final public product.
  • All public figures are drawn from official government or regulator sources; none describe an identifiable individual.
Handling rule. If any personal information is discovered in any file, it is removed and replaced with a placeholder before the next commit, and the discovery is logged in COORDINATION.md.
