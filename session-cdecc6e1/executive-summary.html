Executive Summary

Prepared 2026-11-08 for an anonymized Central Los Angeles Jack in the Box property on an NNN lease expiring 2030-2035. All figures are drawn from verified official sources or are explicitly labelled assumptions; nothing is inferred.

Bottom line (preliminary, pre-tax). The model shows a close call: at an exit cap of about 6.3% the value of holding equals the net proceeds of selling. Below ~6.3% sell now (preferably via a Section 1031 exchange); above ~6.3% holding renews better — provided the tenant renews at market rent, insurance remains available, and the county's projected population decline does not accelerate. Recommended path: extend the lease to a 10+ year WALT now, then sell in 2027-2028 before the 2030-2035 repricing.
