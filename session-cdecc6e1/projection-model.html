  • Pre-tax; owner basis unknown (P05); CPA tax model required before any binding decision (X-06).
Reinvestment check: net proceeds reinvested at the verified 10-year Treasury (4.84%, M53/T12) yield about $107,158/yr at the base case; the directional net-lease anchor is 7.3% (M55, T11) with added concentration risk.
