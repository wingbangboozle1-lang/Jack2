Exit cap-rate bands (sensitivity, not an appraisal)

  • 5.50% — Strong market, long lease, credit tenant. Best-case exit.
  • 6.00% — Healthy single-tenant QSR pricing.
  • 6.50% — Base case used in the model.
  • 7.00% — Rate-stress or short remaining term.
  • 7.50%+ — Distressed / near-expiry / tenant-credit concern.
