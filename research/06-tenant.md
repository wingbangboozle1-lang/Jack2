## JACK on Track (announced April 23, 2025 — company IR press release + 10-K)
- Block closure program: approximately 150–200 underperforming restaurants (majority in system 30+ years);
  press coverage of the release cited 80–120 closures by end of 2025 (company release detail).
- Discontinued dividend (reallocation to debt paydown).
- Significantly reduced spend on company-owned new unit development from 2026.
- "Select real estate sales" to accelerate cash flow.
- Del Taco: strategic alternatives → definitive Stock Purchase Agreement 2025-10-15 to sell Del Taco Holdings
  to Yadav Enterprises, Inc. for $115 million cash, subject to adjustments (pending at filing).
- New CEO: Lance Tucker (CEO since March 2025; interim principal executive officer since Feb 2025).
- Activist situation: proxy contest disclosed in FY2025 10-K risk factors; Nomination and Cooperation
  Agreement with GreenWood Investors, LLC dated 2025-11-03 (exhibit 10.1.28) resolves director nominations.

## Impairment driver quote (MD&A)
Q3 2024 Del Taco impairment triggers included "lower margins due in part to lower sales and higher wages required
in California effective April 1, 2024 under AB 1228 and... inflation and interest rates." Goodwill fully impaired
by end FY2025; Del Taco trademark impaired $177.9M in Q2 2025.

## Credit-relevant observations (analysis, clearly labeled as derived)
1. Tenant/franchise system is contracting (net -55 JIB units FY2025; 150-200 closure program ongoing through ~2027).
2. Two consecutive net-loss years (FY2024, FY2025) driven by impairments; still operating-cash-flow positive.
3. Negative stockholders' equity and ~$1.7B long-term debt — leveraged; dividend eliminated to pay down debt.
4. California concentration (941 JIB units; ~70% of system in CA+TX) ties property performance to CA QSR economics.
5. AB 1228 $20 fast-food minimum wage materially raised labor costs (wage inflation ~7.6% in company restaurants).
6. Franchisee health is the direct payer risk for a franchised-location lease (rent paid by franchisee or by JACK
   under sublease structures; 10-K notes franchisee bankruptcies/closures as a stated risk).
