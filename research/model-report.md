## What this model does NOT do

- It does not value the actual parcel. Rent, expiration date, basis and debt are placeholders (P01-P12).
- It does not compute income tax, depreciation recapture, NIIT or California tax. That requires the owner's basis (P05).
- It does not price tenant default beyond the renewal probability, and it does not model a wildfire or earthquake loss; those are handled in the risk register.
- It does not use any non-official data source. Cap-rate inputs are a labelled assumption, because no government agency publishes asset-level cap rates (M51).
