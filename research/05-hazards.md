## Earthquake (USGS official, UCERF3)
30-year probabilities, Los Angeles region: 60% for M6.7+; 46% for M7.0+; 31% for M7.5+.
LA-area faults include Newport-Inglewood (through Central LA area) per USGS Quaternary Fault database context.

## Insurance market aftermath (official CDI Bulletin 2025-4)
- CA FAIR Plan received 4,794 claims from the Palisades and Eaton fires; had paid $914M to policyholders
  (as of the Feb 2025 bulletin); FAIR Plan estimated total loss ~$4 billion from the two fires.
- CDI Commissioner approved (Order 2025-1) a $1 billion assessment on FAIR Plan member insurers — the first
  in over 30 years; insurers may recoup 50% via policyholder surcharges per Bulletin 2025-4.
- NAIC CIPR (Center for Insurance Policy and Research) 2025 journal estimates total January 2025 LA fires
  losses at ~$40 billion (2024$): https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/cipr-jir-2025-4.pdf (regulator-affiliated source).

## Seismic retrofit ordinances (LADBS official)
- Mandatory Soft-Story Retrofit Program (Ordinances 183893, 184081): applies to wood-frame buildings with
  2+ stories permitted before 1978 with soft/weak/open first story; compliance deadlines: 2 yrs proof/plans,
  3.5 yrs permit, 7 yrs completion from Order to Comply. Includes "Condos/Commercial" in Priority III.
- Typical single-story fast-food buildings are generally out of scope, but any multi-story or older URM
  structure needs a parcel-level check (LADBS retrofit lookups) — flagged pending address.
