## Tax rules relevant to a sale decision
- §1031 (IRS): real-property like-kind exchanges defer gain; post-TCJA applies to real property only;
  US real property is like-kind to other US real property; property held primarily for sale does not qualify.
  Deadlines: identify replacement within 45 days; close within 180 days (Form 8824 instructions).
- CA capital gains (FTB): "California does not have a lower rate for capital gains. All capital gains are taxed
  as ordinary income." (CA PIT rates run to 12.3%, plus 1% mental-health surtax above $1M taxable income —
  top combined 13.3% per CA rate schedules; the surtax detail is documented in FTB/CA rate schedules; cite FTB page
  for the ordinary-income treatment.)
- California conforms to federal §1031 for real property (Form FTB 3840 annual reporting tracks deferred CA gain —
  see FTB form 3840: https://www.ftb.ca.gov/forms/... ; flagged for manual confirmation of current-year form URL).
- Prop 13 (BOE Pub 800-10): 1% general tax rate cap + voter-approved debt; assessed value growth capped at 2%/yr;
  reassessment to market value occurs on change in ownership or new construction. A sale re-bases the buyer's taxes
  at the purchase price — a consideration for pricing/attractiveness to buyers, not for the seller's own carry.
- CA withholding on real property sales (3⅓% of gross/alternative amounts) applies to California sellers —
  FTB Form 597/593 (flagged for manual confirmation via FTB site).
