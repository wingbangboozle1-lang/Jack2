Executive summary

Question: sell the Central Los Angeles Jack in the Box NNN property before the lease expires (stated range 2030-2035), or renew and hold? Answer: neither 'sell tomorrow' nor 'wait and renew'. Extend early or sell with term - never arrive at expiry short.

What is verified (2026-11-12, official sources only)

  • Tenant is contracting: 2,115 restaurants at Jul 5 2026 vs 2,168 a year earlier; system same-store sales -1.1% in Q3 FY2026 and -4.2% year-to-date; FY2025 was also -4.2% (M29-M32, SEC S07/S08).
  • Area demand is flat-to-shrinking: County population 9,694,934, -3.2% since 2020; city -0.8% (M01-M04). Jobs are growing slowly (+0.4% YoY) and unemployment has improved to 4.4% metro / 4.8% county (M21-M23).
  • Capital is expensive: 10-year Treasury 4.88% (M28). Any NNN price must clear that anchor plus a tenant/term spread - the spread itself is unverified and stays blank (B07).
  • Sale friction changed Jul 1 2026: City of LA Measure ULA now bites above $5.4M at 4% on the full price (5.5% above $10.9M), on top of the 0.45% base tax (M35-M36) - if the parcel is inside the city (Q-04, unconfirmed).
  • Hazards are real and recent: USGS 60% chance of M6.7+ in the region in 30 years (M39); the Jan 2025 Eaton + Palisades fires destroyed 16,251 structures in this county (M40-M41).

Recommendation (sequenced)

  1. Days 0-30: Close P1 documents (lease/expiry Q-01, rent Q-02, clauses Q-03, store sales Q-05). No pricing conversation before this.
  2. Days 15-60: Two broker BOVs (as-is and as-if-extended) under NDA (SG-05); insurance market check (SG-07); Prop 13 subsidy quantified privately (SG-09).
  3. Days 30-90: Open blend-and-extend discussion (10-15 years, rent at/above in-place, guaranty reviewed) while the 1031 replacement pipeline is pre-built (SG-02, SG-08).
  4. Decision gate: If extension at/above in-place rent for 10+ years is signed, hold then sell 2029-2032 into the long-term buyer pool (Strategy B).
