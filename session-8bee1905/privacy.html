Privacy

What is deliberately absent from this site, and how the privacy constraint is enforced in code.

No personal information appears anywhere on this site. That is the highest-priority constraint in the brief and it is enforced by code, not by intention: an automated scan fails the build if an email address, phone number, street address or parcel number appears in this session's output.

How the constraint is enforced

  • Code, not intent. The build fails on a match for email, phone, street-address or parcel-number patterns.
  • Nothing private is collected. This workbook was produced without any private document, and it asks for none to be uploaded. The private gaps are listed so they can be closed offline.
  • Anonymised throughout. The property is described only as a Jack in the Box NNN asset around central Los Angeles with a 2030–2035 lease expiry.
  • No third-party trackers. Static pages, no analytics, no external scripts, no cookies.
  • Public agency addresses. Where a source is an agency whose published contact address appears in its own document, that address is not reproduced here; only the document is linked.

If a private figure is needed for a decision

Every private input this analysis needs is listed on the Flagged for review page next to what would close it. Those lookups can be done privately, off this site, and this workbook's conclusions will not change until they are supplied. Nothing has to be entered anywhere.

Internal decision support. Not an appraisal, a tax opinion, legal advice or an offer. No street address, parcel number, owner name, loan number or account appears anywhere on this site.
