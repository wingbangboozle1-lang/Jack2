Requirements that are only partly met, and why

Decide sell or renew

A single verdict is not supportable, because rent, exact expiry, options, basis and debt are private. What is published instead is a decision rule plus six analysed branches, ordered so that the answer resolves as each input arrives. A confident verdict built on invented inputs would be worse than this, not better.

Assess the area economy, including schools

Population, income, jobs, wages, prices and consumer demand are verified at city, county and metro level. School quality is not attached to the property, because attendance areas are drawn by address. Attaching a district statistic to an unknown school would be an inference, and the brief forbids inference.

Build a reliable projection system

A projection framework with explicit inputs and limits is published, and only official series are carried as projections. No rent or price forecast is published, deliberately. See projection limits for the reasoning, stated openly so it can be argued with.

Internal decision support. Not an appraisal, a tax opinion, legal advice or an offer. No street address, parcel number, owner name, loan number or account appears anywhere on this site.

Every published value links to the official source it was read from on 2026-11-10. Values that could not be confirmed are blank and listed on Flagged for review. Reconstruction: python3 session-8bee1905/scripts/build_site.py · verification: verification log.
