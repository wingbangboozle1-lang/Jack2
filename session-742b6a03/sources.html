How to audit this workbook

  1. Pick any row on Market economics.
  2. Click its source id. The official document opens at the publisher's own domain.
  3. Search that document for the value. The value, unit, period and geography are all printed in the row.
  4. If you cannot find it, the row is wrong — report it and it will be corrected or blanked.

Notes on scope

  • State and federal fiscal years differ from calendar years. Jack in the Box FY2025 ended 2025-09-28 and every fiscal figure is labelled with that date.
  • BLS metro data are not seasonally adjusted and are subject to revision; the summary used here was updated 2026-10-05.
  • Census QuickFacts mixes Vintage 2025 population estimates with 2020–2024 ACS 5-year characteristics; the period column says which is which.
  • Rate series are daily and are dated to the observation, not to today.
