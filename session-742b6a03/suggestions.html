Suggestions · review before action

Preserve options now; decide the property only after parcel facts are known

The safest evidence-based next move is a reversible dual track: prepare a sale case and request a nonbinding extension proposal while closing the private lease, parcel, insurance and tax gaps. This is a process recommendation, not an appraisal or a final sell/renew instruction.

Privacy first. Do not upload an owner's name, address, APN, account, contact details, lease, tax return, net worth or tenant contact to this public site. Review confidential documents privately with qualified advisers. Parcel-level hazard and value claims are intentionally withheld.

Current decision stance

Do not choose “sell” or “renew” from the public regional data alone. Keep both options alive, calendar all lease deadlines, and obtain the missing property-specific evidence. Then compare net after-tax proceeds and risk-adjusted hold value from verified inputs. If there is no time to complete diligence before a contractual deadline, get independent legal advice on preserving rights; do not let a model or this page substitute for the lease.

There is no single universally “best” choice without the actual lease, offer, basis, debt, cash needs, risk tolerance and reinvestment plan. The pathway above prioritizes reversibility and wealth preservation while avoiding a fabricated price or personal tax conclusion.

Important distinctions

  • Regional income, population, job growth and school data are contextual signals, not an appraisal, rent forecast or parcel-level demand study. The source link on each row identifies geography, measure and vintage.
  • Official hazard maps are screening tools. Obtain property-specific engineering and insurance underwriting; a county event does not prove the subject parcel is exposed.
  • Tax consequences, including any like-kind exchange, depend on private facts and statutory requirements. Obtain advice from qualified professionals before signing a sale or exchange contract. S16 ↗
