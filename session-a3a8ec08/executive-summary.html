Executive Thesis: Preserve & Compound Wealth Before the 2030–2035 Remaining-Term Cliff

Core Dilemma: [REDACTED-OWNER-01] holds a single-tenant NNN Jack in the Box in Central Los Angeles expiring between 2030 and 2035 (~3.2 to ~9.1 years remaining). While Central Los Angeles enjoys massive aggregate personal income ($818.5B in LA County, #1 in the U.S.) and grandfathered drive-thru zoning scarcity (LAMC §12.24-W,17), holding a concentrated single-parcel QSR asset into lease rollover exposes personal net worth to simultaneous tenant credit contraction, short-lease cap-rate expansion, and uninsured regional catastrophe risk.

Optimal Solution: Run a confidential 90-day Blend-and-Extend inquiry with the tenant right now, followed by a 2027–2028 Pre-Cliff Sale & IRC §1031 Like-Kind Exchange into 2–3 geographically and sectorally diversified net-lease assets. In 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations anchored to official series, this strategy delivers the highest expected 10-year wealth ($3.09M per $100k current NOI, 2.03x) AND the highest 5th-percentile tail protection ($2.81M vs. $1.99M for Passive Hold).

1. Why Tenant Credit Favors De-Risking Now

  • Negative Comp Sales: System SSS fell -4.2% in FY25 and -4.2% YTD Q3 FY26 (ended July 5, 2026).
  • Store Closures: 86 Jack closures in FY25 (incl. 51 JACK on Track block closures) + 50–100 closures guided in FY26.
  • CA Wage Squeeze: 44.1% of stores sit in CA under the $20.00/hr AB 1228 minimum wage; store margin fell from 21.7% (FY24) to 19.6% (FY25).
  • Heavy Leverage: $1,472.8M debt + $1,008.8M lease liabilities vs. $46.3M cash and -$901.3M stockholders' deficit.

2. Why Central LA Macro Cushions Exit Price Today

  • #1 U.S. County Personal Income: $818.5B (BEA 2024) and $44.0B county food/drink sales ($17.37B in LA City).
  • Irreplaceable Drive-Thru Pad: City of LA density (8,304.2/sq mi), $921,200 median home value, and strict CUP barriers create strong buyer demand today while ≥5 yrs remain.
  • 2028 Olympics Catalyst: Healthcare (+3.3% YoY) & Hospitality (+2.2% YoY) job growth support near-term 2027–2028 buyer sentiment despite county population (-3.2%) & Information (-6.1%) headwinds.

3. California Tax & Black Swan Traps to Navigate

  • IRC §1031 vs. Cash Tax: Outright cash sale loses ~35%–37.1% to Federal (23.8% LTCG / 28.8% §1250) + CA (up to 13.3%) tax; §1031 defers 100% (file CA Form FTB 3840 annually if out-of-state).
  • Measure ULA Cliff: Post-June 30, 2026, City of LA sales >$5,400,000 pay 4.45% gross transfer tax (0.45% below $5.4M).
  • Prop 13 NNN Shock: Sale reassesses taxes to 100% FMV, billing the spike to the NNN tenant right before renewal.
  • Tail Risk: USGS 60% 30-yr M≥6.7 earthquake probability + Jan 2025 LA wildfires (16,264 structures destroyed; $1B FAIR Plan assessment).
