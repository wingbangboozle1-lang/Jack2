Which lease clauses decide the answer

Clause to audit before any decision
Option rent: fixed step-up vs CPI vs fair market value with arbitration - determines who captures the 2030-2035 re-pricing
Option notice deadline and consequence of missing it (a missed window can forfeit renewal)
Percentage rent breakpoint and reporting obligations
Assignment/sublease consent standard and recapture rights
Tenant termination or go-dark rights, and any co-tenancy condition
Landlord obligations that could erode NNN purity (roof, structure, parking lot, HVAC)
Environmental indemnity and existing-recognition of any dry-cleaner/fuel contamination nearby
Condemnation and casualty provisions - critical after the 2025 fire losses and for seismic events
Estoppel and SNDA requirements
Guaranty by franchisee parent and any personal guaranty (personally identifying details must never be published)

What the tenant's own filing tells you about renewal

The good news

“We have generally been able to renew our restaurant leases as they expire at then-current market rates.” S9 ↗ Franchise agreements run 20 years, so a franchisee that has invested in the brand usually wants to stay. S9 ↗

The bad news

The system shrank by 55 units in FY2025, a block-closure program is running, AUV fell 3.8%, Del Taco is being sold, the dividend is gone, and ~$1.7B of securitized debt carries gross-sales covenants. S9 ↗ A franchisee under that pressure is a weaker credit at exactly the moment you want a higher rent.
