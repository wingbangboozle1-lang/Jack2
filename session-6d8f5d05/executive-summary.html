Executive Summary

Sell or Renew? — Hold the Option Open, Then Price It

Bottom line first, then the reasoning, then the numbers that decide it.

Recommendation

Do not renew on autopilot and do not sell on fear. Run a sequenced plan: (1) assemble the 12 missing documents, (2) verify parcel hazards and insurance, (3) negotiate a lease extension with a market-rent reset, (4) obtain an appraisal, and (5) sell into a §1031 exchange only if the cap rate ≤6.00% or the replacement yield clears 6.97%. Hold if cap rate >7.00%.

Why This Is the Answer

The two paths are close

At the benchmark rent, holding produces ~$5.69M of 2040 wealth; selling and redeploying at the verified 7.3% net-lease benchmark produces ~$5.94M. The gap is about 4% — inside the error bars. The decision turns on facts not yet supplied.

Source: S10

Only one action improves both outcomes

A market-rent reset needs just +7.1% to make holding beat selling. It simultaneously raises the price a buyer will pay because the WALT extends. The tenant has structural incentive: its franchise term is 20 years while this lease ends 2030-2035.

Source: S9

Selling is defensible if capital is redeployed

You need 6.97% on proceeds to match holding. Realty Income reported 7.3% on 2025 acquisitions — a thin but positive margin. Section 1031 lets you defer gain and diversify one parcel into several.

Sources: S10, S16

Holding has a real tax advantage

Prop 13 caps assessed value increases at 2%/year while CPI runs at 3.6%. That's a genuine, permanent, non-transferable benefit. A sale resets the basis entirely.

Sources: S6, S1

90-Day Action Plan

  1. Days 1-90: Pull twelve documents (P01-P12). Nothing is decision-grade until rent, expiry, basis, and debt are known.
  2. Days 1-120: Order parcel-level hazard and insurance verification: FEMA, CAL FIRE, CGS seismic, current quote.
  3. Days 90-180: Open extension conversation with franchisee. Ask for market reset with floor, 10-year extension, guaranty.
  4. Days 180-365: Obtain broker opinion of value. If cap ≤6.00%, sell. If cap >7.00%, hold and extend.
  5. If selling: Engage QI before listing. Identify replacement in 45 days. Close in 180. Buy ≥3 properties.
  6. If holding: Build 12-month NOI reserve. Re-test decision every 24 months.
