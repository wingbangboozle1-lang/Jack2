Black Swan Scenarios

RK21: Major metropolitan wildfire event (repeat of January 2025 at scale)

Why: Calibration: Eaton + Palisades destroyed 16,251 structures, with $5.2B-$10.1B of lost output and 47,000 workers affected (S13). A central Los Angeles event of that scale is a low-probability, total-loss-class scenario.

Mitigation: Insurance adequacy is the only real mitigation; confirm limits, deductibles, and insurer rating. Hold a liquidity reserve outside the property.

Residual risk: Medium

RK22: Major earthquake (M7+) in the Los Angeles basin

Why: Regional hazard is documented (S18); no official probability is asserted for the parcel here.

Mitigation: Seismic evaluation, earthquake insurance with a deductible the owner can fund, and a pre-negotiated restoration plan with the tenant.

Residual risk: Medium

RK23: Brand-level shock (food safety, franchisee system failure, brand exit from California)

Why: The tenant discloses food-safety, supply-chain, and franchisee-relations risks, and is mid-restructuring (Del Taco sale, dividend eliminated, ~$1.7B securitized debt) (S9).

Mitigation: Do not grant a below-market long extension without a guaranty; keep a re-tenanting plan for a generic drive-thru box.

Residual risk: Medium

RK24: Civil unrest, pandemic closure, or extended utility shut-off

Why: The tenant names civil unrest and severe weather as risk factors (S9); 2020 closures showed how fast restaurant revenue can go to zero.

Mitigation: Rent-loss and civil-commotion coverage; reserve; lease language on abatement triggers so the landlord is not forced to grant free rent by default.

Residual risk: Low
