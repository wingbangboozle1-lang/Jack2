Methodology

normalization

Initial annual contractual base rent R0 = 100 monetary units. This is a scale convention, not a property rent estimate. Results are multiples of actual verified R0 once available.

timing

120 end-of-month cash flows over ten years. Annual effective discount and rent escalation are converted by exponentiation, not divided by twelve. Re-letting cost is paid at the expiry month; downtime begins after expiry. These are modeling conventions, not observed lease terms.

post expiry

Renewal resets rent relative to initial R0, with no downtime or releasing cost in this idealized path. Re-letting applies a reset, downtime and releasing cost. Vacant paths retain only an explicit residual-rent-multiple hypothesis. Renewal is not unilateral: tenant consent and executed documentation are required.

valuation

Unlevered, pre-owner-income-tax property value. Terminal value = final-year net rent / exit cap, less terminal selling cost; vacant path uses residual hypothesis. Compare with gross sale price less selling costs BEFORE debt payoff and owner income taxes. Then separately test the owner's actual after-debt, after-tax equity proceeds and reinvestment alternatives.

interpretation

Scenarios are conditional stress tests, not probability-weighted expected values, confidence intervals, market cap-rate estimates or forecasts of this property's rent. No annual interpolation of official five-year averages is performed. Regional earthquake probabilities are not building-loss or tenant-default probabilities.

Limitations

  • County, metropolitan and school-district geographies differ; none is a verified trade-area or parcel analysis.
  • Official-source status does not make commissioned impact studies, issuer filings or university forecasts government statistical observations.
  • Source excerpts are curated selections checked against fetched issuing pages, not a live data feed. Publication dates and revisions must be rechecked before a transaction.
  • No independent appraisal, broker opinion, legal review, engineering report, environmental assessment, insurance quote or tenant underwriting was performed.
  • Automated checks validate internal consistency, citations, privacy guards and calculations; they do not prove the underlying investment thesis.
  • The public repository and site contain no property address or owner/tenant personal identifiers. Public repository coordinates are project metadata, not an assertion about ownership.
