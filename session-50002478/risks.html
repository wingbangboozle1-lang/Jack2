Risk register: ordinary cycles and rare shocks

30 qualitative pathways. Likelihood and loss are not quantified unless an official source explicitly provides a regional measure; no parcel-level probability is invented.

Tail risks are not diversified away by a NNN label

Earthquake, wildfire, flood, insurance failure, pandemic, civil unrest, utility/cyber outage and compound macro shocks can interrupt rent or impair residual value even when the tenant survives. Test liquidity and rent-loss coverage, not just headline occupancy.
