Decision

Sell a single-tenant Jack in the Box (NNN, Central Los Angeles, expiry window 2030-2035) before the lease expires, or renew and hold — for an owner whose priorities are wealth preservation with a manageable level of risk and room for growth.

Normalization: The owner's contract rent, basis and debt are private and blank (flags F-01, F-05). Every number below is therefore published on an index basis: value = 1.00 equals one year of current net operating income (NOI). Dollar outcomes scale linearly with actual NOI. No private fact was assumed.

S-EXCHANGE
Same timing as SELL, but replacement property of equal or greater value is acquired within the 45/180-day framework (S16), deferring federal capital-gains tax; transfer tax still applies at sale.

Holding wins on expected wealth (+8.3 index units) only because the asset's ~6.8% yield exceeds the officially anchored 4.86% reinvestment anchor. Selling/exchanging wins on risk-adjusted wealth: the worst-5% outcome is 5.1 index units better, and the chance of an idiosyncratic loss above 10 index units falls from 8.9% to 0.01%. The break-even reinvestment rate for the base 2032 case is 7.06%: below that, holding is the expected-value winner; above it, selling wins outright. The user's stated objective (wealth preservation with growth at manageable risk) is therefore served by ranking the risk-adjusted winner first, with the hold case retained as the expected-value benchmark and the triggers as the switching rules.

Conditions & falsifiers

  • If the best bid (net of 4.45%/5.95% City transfer stack and closing costs) exceeds hold-value at the model's base break-even cap, sell/exchange.
  • If the tenant offers renewal with escalators >= metro ECI (3.9% YoY, S01) and the renewal term runs past 2032, hold through the extension and re-price in 2030.
  • If tenant SSS falls again for a second consecutive fiscal year, or guidance turns to net system closures above 100/yr, move to rank 1 immediately regardless of price (R01, R02).
  • If earthquake coverage cannot be bound at acceptable cost after any fault-map finding, exit via rank 1 (R17, R19).
  • The recommendation is wrong if the owner's actual basis makes the after-tax cost of selling exceed the single-asset risk premium by a wide margin (basis is blank — F-05/F-10).
  • The recommendation is wrong if the parcel is outside City of Los Angeles limits (no ULA applies — F-08), which materially lowers exit friction and favors selling sooner.
  • The recommendation is wrong if the tenant's next fiscal-year SSS turns positive and the closure program ends (R01/R02 reverse).
  • The recommendation is wrong if the official net-lease benchmark yield moves materially away from 6.8% before execution (S07 is a dated print, 2026-11-02).
