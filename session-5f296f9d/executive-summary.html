Conditions and falsifiers

  • If the best bid (net of 4.45%/5.95% City transfer stack and closing costs) exceeds hold-value at the model's base break-even cap, sell/exchange.
  • If the tenant offers renewal with escalators >= metro ECI (3.9% YoY, S01) and the renewal term runs past 2032, hold through the extension and re-price in 2030.
  • If tenant SSS falls again for a second consecutive fiscal year, or guidance turns to net system closures above 100/yr, move to rank 1 immediately regardless of price (R01, R02).
  • If earthquake coverage cannot be bound at acceptable cost after any fault-map finding, exit via rank 1 (R17, R19).
  • The recommendation is wrong if the owner's actual basis makes the after-tax cost of selling exceed the single-asset risk premium by a wide margin (basis is blank — F-05/F-10).
  • The recommendation is wrong if the parcel is outside City of Los Angeles limits (no ULA applies — F-08), which materially lowers exit friction and favors selling sooner.
  • The recommendation is wrong if the tenant's next fiscal-year SSS turns positive and the closure program ends (R01/R02 reverse).
  • The recommendation is wrong if the official net-lease benchmark yield moves materially away from 6.8% before execution (S07 is a dated print, 2026-11-02).
