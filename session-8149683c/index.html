The recommendation, stated as a rule

Hold and extend if your required return is below the sell/hold crossover. Sell if it is above.

At a 7.10% market cap rate and six years of remaining term, that crossover is an owner return of 7.39%. At a 7.85% cap rate it is 8.15%.

A single-asset QSR with a near-term roll is normally bought by private capital with a required return below that crossover. If that describes you, the arithmetic says hold. If you require a higher return than the market is paying, the arithmetic says sell now. Full decision analysis →
