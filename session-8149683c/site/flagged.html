4. What to send to close the gaps

Tier D documents

  • The executed lease, all amendments, and every side letter
  • The guaranty, if any, and the tenant's legal entity name
  • Three years of rent invoices and the current rent roll
  • The tenant's sales reports, if the lease requires them
  • The loan agreement, current balance and prepayment terms
  • Ownership entity documents and the depreciation schedule
  • The current insurance binder and any loss runs
  • Title commitment and the most recent Phase I ESA

Then re-run

The engine takes remaining_years, base_rent and the rest as arguments. Once the lease is in hand, replace the axis sweep with the real values and rerun python3 model/project_model.py. The site regenerates from the same JSON and every affected row flips from UNVERIFIED to VERIFIED with a document citation.

Until that happens, the recommendation in this dossier is conditioned on the unknowns and is stated as a rule rather than as a verdict.
