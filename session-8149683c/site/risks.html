The single most important asymmetry

A sale transfers every risk on this page to the buyer at the moment of closing. Holding retains all of them. That statement is true regardless of the cap rate, and it is why an owner whose required return is anywhere near the market cap rate should weigh risk transfer explicitly rather than treating it as a tiebreaker.

How the black swans are priced

The projection engine overlays a shock drawn independently per trial at 1.65% per ten-year hold, with severity drawn uniformly between a 35–85% rent interruption and a 20–45% permanent loss of exit value. A sale completed before the event is left untouched; a held asset takes the full hit. This is the only place in the model where the four decisions are treated asymmetrically, and it is treated asymmetrically because the world is asymmetric.

What this register does not cover

Legal title defects, easements, an unrecorded encroachment, a tenant's purchase option, a radius restriction that blocks another QSR operator from the box, and environmental contamination. All of these sit in the owner's document set (P-14, P-15) and none of them were supplied. They are the reason a Phase I ESA and a title commitment should precede any sale.
