Projection System — LA County Economy & Asset Value, 2026–2035

A transparent, formula-driven model. Every assumption is labeled with its source or marked as an assumption. Nothing here is a guarantee; it is a decision-support frame to compare "sell now" vs "renew" under consistent scenarios.

Read first. Value figures for this property use placeholders: NOI = [NOI]. An illustrative $300,000 NOI is used only to show arithmetic (marked Computed). Replace with actuals before relying on any number. Market cap-rate levels are not published by any official source — scenario bands are assumptions and are flagged accordingly.

Model logic

Asset value (t) = NOI(t) ÷ Cap rate(t) NOI(t) = NOI(2026) × (1 + g_NOI)^(t − 2026) g_NOI = observed escalation schedule, or LA-County inflation proxy (assumption flagged) Cap rate(t) = in-place ± scenario shift (NNN QSR band 6.0%–7.5% assumed; NOT an official figure) Economy index = chain of annual real-GDP growth rates (2024 = 100)

Escalation and any re-leasing costs are lease-specific; obtain the actual lease abstract before final analysis.

Read-across. Rate moves dominate the outcome: ±100 bps of cap rate ≈ ∓13–19% of value — far larger than a decade of 2.5% NOI growth (+25%). The decision hinges on (a) securing escalations and (b) the rate environment at sale/renewal — not on population growth. A lease with weak escalations should be renegotiated or sold while NNN demand is deep.
