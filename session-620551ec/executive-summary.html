Bottom line

Best course of action: negotiate a lease extension/renewal FIRST (10–20 yr, CPI- or fixed-escalated, repriced at or above the current NNN rent), then decide sell-vs-hold on the repriced contract and fresh market pricing — using the written triggers T1–T6. Do not list blind; do not renew blind; never let the asset run dark into 2030–2035.

Confidence: moderate. The answer is a sequence, not an unconditional price — the inputs that would fix the price (exact rent, jurisdiction/Measure ULA status, cap rate, basis) are owner-private or non-official and are deliberately blank.

The plan (sequence, not a leap)

  1. Weeks 1–4 — Evidence: exact lease date/options/rent (Q01–Q03, Q16); jurisdiction + ULA exposure (Q04); assessed value/Prop 13 (Q05); CAL FIRE/USGS parcel lookup (Q17); insurance quotes (Q18).
  2. Weeks 3–6 — Pricing: broker opinion of value; rent benchmark vs Central LA QSR pads (G13); CPA after-tax comparison: sale vs renew-then-sell vs 1031 (G04).
  3. Weeks 6–12 — Negotiate: extension/renewal first — repriced rent, CPI escalations, guarantee + security, continuous-operation clause, options structure (G08).
  4. Week 12 — Decision meeting: re-run sell-vs-hold on actual numbers; apply T1–T6; document the decision and the re-review date (12 months pre-expiry).
  5. Ongoing: quarterly tenant check (8-K/10-K), monthly DGS10 + EDD, annual ULA indexation review each July.

Why not the other paths (plainly)

  • Sell blind today: you would price off incomplete information and pay the ULA cliff if in-city — irreversible.
  • Renew blind: flat or below-market rent locked for a decade — quiet wealth leak.
  • Hold to a dark expiry: the worst risk-adjusted path — sale into vacancy at the bottom of the negotiation cycle.
