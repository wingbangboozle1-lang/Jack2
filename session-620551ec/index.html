The decision, in one paragraph

The asset is a single-tenant Jack in the Box on an NNN lease expiring somewhere in 2030–2035, “around central Los Angeles.” The verified macro picture is a slow, services-led economy: LA County unemployment 4.8% and improving (S09), population shrinking -3.2% since 2020 (S03), $818.5B of personal income (2024, largest in the US, S07), a resilient port complex (S15), an elevated 10Y Treasury at 4.88% (S02), and a tenant in a managed shrink — same-store sales -1.1%, system at 2,115 restaurants (-53 y/y), franchise-level margin 37.4% (S05). None of this tells you what the property is worth. It does tell you how to decide: negotiate the extension first, then decide with the actual numbers (triggers T1–T6 on the Decision page).

What this site refuses to do: invent a sale price, a cap rate, the lease’s rent or expiry, the owner’s basis, or the parcel’s identity. Those are blank in the data ledger — flagged, not fabricated (see Flagged for review).

How to read this workbook

  • Executive summary — the answer on one page.
  • Decision — four strategies scored, T1–T6 triggers, and the recommended sequence.
  • Market data — every verified figure with its official source link.
  • Projection model — deterministic value grids (rent growth × cap rate × expiry 2030–2035) and a black-swan overlay.
  • Risk register — BAU + black-swan risks mapped to sell / hold / renew / extend.
  • Questions & answers — what must be answered, what is known, and how to find the missing information.
  • Suggestions — a review queue to improve this analysis before implementing anything.
  • Flagged for review — everything unverified or irregular, with the source links.
