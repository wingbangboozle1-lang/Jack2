How to read this model

The workbook refuses to invent the subject property's inputs. The model is therefore index-based: rent index 100, value = rent ÷ cap rate. Plug the owner's actual rent and the broker's cap rate into the same formulas for live numbers. All “analyst assumptions” are labeled and are not official data.

Method: deterministic; index-based; no invented property inputs · Generated 2026-11-12 by scripts/model.py (deterministic, stdlib only, no network).

Black-swan overlay (analyst assumptions — labeled, not official)

Sum of probability × impact = expected annual value drag ≈ 1.35% of asset value per year. An owner who cannot bear that carry should not hold.

Sum of analyst annual_prob x value_impact. Compares the carry of holding vs selling; an owner who cannot bear a ~1.4%/yr expected drag should not hold.

Interactive use (no data leaves your browser)

Inputs are owner-supplied at the decision meeting. Example: rent $180,000/yr, broker cap 6.5% → value ≈ $2.77M; at 7.0% ≈ $2.57M. Escalated 3%/yr to 2032, the same cap yields ≈ $3.28M before any exit tax. Measure ULA (if in-city) then takes 4–5.5% of gross on the way out.
