Privacy — non-negotiable, by design

  • Absent from this repository and this site, forever: owner name, property address, APN/parcel, loan/account numbers, tenant-contact personal details, owner signatures, tax returns.
  • Internal only: any personal information encountered during research (e.g., public filings naming officers) is summarized at the corporate/entity level and never attached to the subject property.
  • Blank by default: owner-specific facts (rent, basis, assessed value, exact expiry) are blank until the owner supplies them privately.
  • Built-in enforcement: the generator fails the build if PII-like patterns (SSN/phone/email/card/APN markers) appear on any page; the verifier scans the whole folder; CI re-runs both.
  • Suggestion selections on the Suggestions page persist only in your own browser's localStorage; nothing is transmitted anywhere.
  • No tracking: no analytics, cookies, pixels, or third-party scripts. The only outbound links are source citations.
  • Corrections: if you believe personal information appears anywhere in this workbook, open an issue at the repository and it will be removed and logged in COORDINATION.md.
