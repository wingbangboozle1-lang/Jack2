Decision framing

Decision is NOT 'sell vs renew' as a coin flip. It is: (1) secure maximum information and leverage first (exact lease terms, jurisdiction/ULA determination, broker opinion of value, CPA after-tax model), (2) negotiate the renewal/extension FIRST because it is cheap and reprices the asset either way, (3) decide only when triggers fire or the negotiation lands. Numbers below are structural — the workbook refuses to invent a sale price (inputs are blank by design).

Recommendation: Negotiate the extension first, then price it — do not sell blind, do not renew blind.
With DGS10 at 4.88% (2026-11-09), LA County unemployment 4.8% and improving, county population shrinking, and the tenant in a managed shrink (~93% franchised, SSS -1.1%, -53 restaurants y/y), central tendency favors a repriced renewal IF rent can be benchmarked up or held flat with strong escalations. Selling now is rational only if the opinion of value clears the owner's after-tax hurdle, the ULA exposure (if in-city) is acceptable, and a trigger fires. Holding to a dark expiry is the worst risk-adjusted path. The decision is made on the negotiation outcome + T1-T6, expected in 2026-2027, not on a static 2026 guess.

Confidence: moderate — bounded by the blank/flagged inputs (exact lease, jurisdiction/ULA, cap rate, rent). The recommendation is a SEQUENCE, not a coin-flip price.

What would change it: A renewal offer with below-market flat rent → switch weight to A. A cap-rate compression wave (DGS10 < 4%) → A becomes clearly superior. A confirmed tenant bankruptcy risk → A immediately. Jurisdiction confirmed OUTSIDE City of LA (no ULA) → A improves materially.

Written triggers — the decision is made when one fires, not before

#TriggerAction
T1Opinion of value < owner's after-tax hurdle rate of returnSell path A becomes primary; renew only if negotiation raises rent materially
T2Cap rate moves > 75bps adverse (or DGS10 > 5.5% sustained 60 days)Re-price; if sale no longer clears hurdle, shift weight to C
T3Tenant closes stores / margins fall 2 consecutive quarters (watch 8-Ks and 10-K)Accelerate exit (A) or require additional security on renewal
T4Renewal negotiation fails or stalls past a written deadlineList for sale (A) with 6-12 month runway before any dark date
T5Owner concentration > 35% of net worth in this asset OR hard liquidity needSell (A) or 1031 exchange into diversified replacement (G04/G09)
T6Insurance quote for renewal term exceeds 1.5× current cost or coverage is deniedRe-run economics; may force A (uninsurable asset is unsaleable to lenders)
