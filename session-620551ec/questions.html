Questions that must be answered before deciding — and how to get the answers

This register is the missing-information discovery system: each row says what is known from verified official sources, what is missing, how critical it is, and the exact acquisition method. Suggestions to discover missing information are part of the task — answers are not invented.

Decision cannot be made reasonably while any “critical” row is open. Criticals: Q01–Q04, Q06, Q07, Q15, Q21.
