One-page advisor summary

A printable page for advisers: where the record stands, the first questions to answer, and what is blocked.

Status: no sell-or-renew choice is supported yet. The calculator applies its arithmetic to the inputs the owner enters, and those inputs are not all verified. No winner is selected. Decision support only, not tax, legal or investment advice.

Verified record

397 values are published, each with its official link (360 verified, 37 computed from verified values). 44 are blank and flagged because they could not be confirmed from an official source.

Answer these first (Critical questions)

  1. Q01: On what exact date does the lease expire?
  2. Q02: What is the rent today, and does it rise during the term?
  3. Q03: Does the tenant hold renewal options: how many, at what rent, with what notice window?
  4. Q04: Who is the tenant: the company, a subsidiary or a franchisee, and is there a guaranty?
  5. Q05: Is the lease fully net: who pays taxes, insurance, roof, structure and parking lot?
  6. Q06: What happens under the lease after a fire, earthquake or condemnation?
  7. Q09: Will the tenant sign an early extension if asked now?
  8. Q10: How is this restaurant trading: yearly sales and rent as a share of sales?
  9. Q13: What would the property sell for today as it stands, and with an extension signed?
  10. Q16: What is the owner's tax basis, and how much depreciation has been taken?
  11. Q18: What does the owner need from this asset: income, a time horizon, and how much loss is tolerable?
  12. Q27: Is the parcel in a mapped fire, flood, fault, liquefaction or landslide zone?
  13. Q29: What insurance is in force, does it cover earthquake, and who pays?

Blocked items (need an input not in the public record)

  • W-01 Replace city and county proxies with trade-area figures. Needs: Parcel location, used only in a secure workflow (private input).
  • W-04 Parcel-level hazard screening. Needs: Parcel location in a secure workflow; private engineering and insurance reports.
  • F-02 Neighbourhood-level data around the parcel. Needs: Parcel location in a secure workflow.
  • F-04 Price the short-lease discount from real quotes. Needs: Quotes from brokers or buyers. No official source publishes these, so none is linked.
  • F-05 Tract-level trend evidence alongside the regional figures. Needs: Tract selection, which needs the parcel location (W-01).
  • F-07 Use the location only in a secure workflow to select Census tracts and local planning, traffic, school and competitor evidence. Needs: Parcel location in a secure workflow.
  • F-11 Replace regional hazard context with the parcel's zone status for earthquake, fire and flood. Needs: Parcel location in a secure workflow.
  • F-14 Commission a private lease abstract and reconcile it with the notice calendar. Needs: Owner's lease documents, read by an authorised adviser (private).

How to use the calculator

Open Sell-versus-hold calculator in a browser. It runs locally, saves nothing and makes no network call. Enter only verified figures; the tax rules it applies are on that page with links to the official text.
