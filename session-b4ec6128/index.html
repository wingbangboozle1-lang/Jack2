Bottom line (analysis)

Recommended course: pursue an early lease renewal/extension first (target ≥10 years, CPI-banded escalations), with a hard fallback to selling — via a §1031 exchange — while ≥5–7 years of lease term remain if renewal cannot be secured on market-competitive terms. Do not hold through the 2030–2035 expiry without a renewal in hand.

  • Why renew first: value for single-tenant NNN is driven by lease term × tenant credit × cap rate. The tenant's credit trajectory is weakening (verified: net losses, negative equity, 150–200-unit closure program), but its own SEC filing states it "generally… renews restaurant leases as they expire at then-current market rates," and the lease carries cost-of-living adjustments in a ~3–3.7% CPI environment.
  • Why the sell fallback has a deadline: each year closer to expiry shortens the weighted-average lease term buyers will pay for; a vacant single-tenant box is the worst outcome in the risk matrix.
  • Wealth preservation: California taxes capital gains as ordinary income (no preferential rate — FTB), so a direct sale bears heavy tax friction; §1031 defers federal and California gain.
  • Black-swan exposure: 60% chance of a M≥6.7 earthquake in the region within 30 years (USGS); January 2025 fires triggered the first FAIR Plan assessment in 30+ years (CDI) — insurance carry-cost risk is real and rising.

How this site was built (verification rules)

  • Every table row carries a link to an official source (government, SEC, or the company's own investor-relations/SEC-filed material where noted).
  • If a value could not be confirmed from an official source, it is left blank and flagged Unverified — nothing is inferred or filled in.
  • Computed values (growth rates, scenario projections) are marked Computed and show the official inputs and formula used.
  • Opinions, ratings, and recommendations are marked as Analysis and are never presented as sourced facts.
  • Personal information is anonymized with placeholders (e.g., [PROPERTY ADDRESS]) and never appears on this site.
