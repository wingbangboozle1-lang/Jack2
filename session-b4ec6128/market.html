Official long-range projections (the projection backbone)

SCAG (Southern California Association of Governments — the region's federally designated metropolitan planning organization) adopted Connect SoCal 2024, whose growth forecast is the official long-range projection for Los Angeles County. SCAG itself notes that long-range forecasts "embrace the uncertainty inherent in long-range forecasting" and are built on low/baseline/high scenarios; the baseline is shown.
