Projection system (method + scenarios)

Method

Two formulas, fully transparent, using only official inputs:

  • NOIt = Current annual NOI × (1 + g)t, where g = the lease's cost-of-living escalation. Scenario bands anchored to official LA CPI: Low 2.0% / Base 3.0% / High 3.7% (recent 12-month LA CPI prints range 2.9–3.7% — BLS). Assumption — actual escalation terms are in the private lease abstract.
  • Valuet = NOIt ÷ cap rate, with the cap rate tested across 4.50%–7.25%. Assumption — no official source publishes cap rates; the band is an illustrative sensitivity, not a forecast. Verify with broker opinions at decision time.
  • Area-demand context: LA County households are officially projected to grow ~+1.0%/yr to 2030 and employment +0.27%/yr to 2050 (SCAG), supporting rent-market fundamentals without relying on them for arithmetic.

Recommendation and decision rules

Recommended course (analysis): Option B — negotiate an early renewal now (target ≥10 years, CPI-banded escalations, corporate guarantee where possible), then choose to hold the income stream or sell with a long lease term. Fallback: sell via §1031 while ≥5–7 years of term remain if renewal terms are not market-competitive by the deadline. Reject Option C (passive hold through expiry) as the risk-dominant path.

Why (from the verified record)

  • The tenant renews as a matter of practice — its SEC filing says so verbatim (10-K, Item 2) — and California is the brand's core market (941 restaurants; 10-K), so an extension ask from a landlord with 4–9 years of term remaining is commercially reasonable.
  • But the tenant's credit trajectory is negative (net losses, negative equity, 150–200-unit closure program, dividend eliminated — Tenant page), so the owner's exposure should shrink over time, not grow: extension improves exit economics; it does not eliminate credit risk.
  • The area's fundamentals do not force a panic sale: long-run household and employment growth are positive (SCAG), the labor market is at 4.4% unemployment with growing food-service employment (BLS), and CPI supports the escalators.
  • Tail risks argue against an open-ended hold: 60%/30-yr earthquake probability (USGS) and insurance hardening (CDI).
  • Preservation of wealth: a direct sale bears federal capital-gains tax plus California tax at ordinary-income rates (FTB); §1031 defers both for real-property exchanges (IRS), with 45/180-day mechanics (Form 8824).

Watch-list triggers (any two should activate the sell branch)

  • This location appears in a closure announcement or goes dark.
  • Tenant or franchisee financial distress (bankruptcy filing, rent delinquency, default notice).
  • JIB same-store sales remain negative for more than 8 consecutive quarters (currently −3.7% FY2025 — 10-K).
  • Renewal offer freezes or caps escalations below the CPI band, or shifts costs back to the owner.
  • Insurance/tax pass-through disputes or material unreimbursed carry-cost increases.
  • Comparable-NNN cap rates exceed ~6.5% (non-official market observation — verify with brokers at decision time).

Balance for the owner as an individual

  • Preservation: §1031 deferral; Prop 13 basis retained while holding (BOE); NNN pass-through of taxes/insurance/maintenance; an explicit earthquake-insurance decision rather than a default.
  • Growth: CPI-banded escalations (~3%/yr base case); area household growth (+1.0%/yr to 2030 — SCAG); redevelopment optionality (zoning unverified — flagged).
  • Manageable-risk profile: extended WALT + explicit quake/insurance review + staged exit checkpoints (reassess at each 5-year WALT threshold) + no over-concentration in one weakening tenant.

Black-swan stress notes

  • Earthquake (quantified): 60% M≥6.7 within 30 years (USGS). Post-event, a leased NNN asset's value depends on building integrity, insurance response, and tenant continuation — all of which favor shorter holding periods or explicit coverage.
  • Insurance cascade (precedent): Jan 2025 fires → $1B FAIR Plan assessment (first in 30+ years) and 50% recoupment surcharges (CDI Bulletin 2025-4) — a permanent-cost repricing channel that reaches unburned areas.
  • Pandemic-style shock (precedent): SCAG region lost 1.9M jobs in ~3 months in 2020; unemployment peaked at 17.3% (SCAG). NNN rent collection held up better in QSR than in most retail segments, but the tail is real.
  • Civil unrest / other shocks: historically present in LA (e.g., 1992); not officially quantified here — treated as an unquantified tail, another argument for diversification over concentration.
