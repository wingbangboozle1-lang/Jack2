Underlying forecast modelUniform California Earthquake Rupture Forecast 3 (UCERF3)—USGS Fact Sheet 2015-3009

Analysis A damaging regional earthquake is a when, not if, at these probabilities — it is the black-swan-tier event with the best official quantification. Implications: (1) the owner's earthquake-insurance decision should be explicit, not a default; (2) NNN leases pass property insurance through to the tenant, but coverage scope, deductibles, and business-interruption outcomes differ; (3) holding-period exposure compounds the longer the asset is held, which factors into the hold vs. sell balance.
