What this workbook is. Eight agent sessions have already published work in this repository. This one does not replace any of them. It re-verifies the decision-critical facts from the original official publishers on 2026-11-08, audits the published workbook line by line, and adds the one thing that was missing: an honest treatment of the fact that the answer sits inside the noise.

The decision, in one page

The financial comparison does not resolve. At the net-lease yield the published model uses (7.3%, Realty Income FY2025) selling wins by -$0.25M of 2040 wealth. At the newest SEC-filed yield (6.8%, Q3 2026, filed 2026-11-02) holding wins by $0.13M. The flip point is 6.97%, which sits between those two official numbers.

Two independent facts therefore decide this asset, and neither is a market forecast:

  • The eight owner facts are missing. Contract rent, exact expiry, escalations, renewal options, basis, debt, recovery structure and parcel geometry. Every model in this repository, including this one, substitutes an assumption for each. Until they exist, any buying or selling decision is being made on a placeholder.
  • The hazard tail is asymmetric. Holding one parcel puts a 58th-percentile loss on the table; the exchanged path puts 21%. The mean barely moves; the tail moves a lot.

The recommendation follows from those two facts, not from the spread: do not choose between selling and renewing yet. Fix the lease first — a market reset plus term plus a guaranty raises both the sale price and the hold economics — and decide with real numbers in hand. Detail on Decision.
