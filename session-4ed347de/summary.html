Executive summary

The recommendation, the reasoning, the action windows, and the conditions that would reverse it.

Recommendation. Do not sell now and do not simply renew on the option. Negotiate an extension with a fair-market rent reset, a 10-year term and a guaranty, then price the asset. If an appraisal later implies a cap rate at or below about 6.0% — or a replacement net-lease book yielding 7.0% or better is actually available to you — exchange into it. If the parcel turns out to be uninsurable, or sits in a very-high fire hazard or mapped liquefaction zone, reduce the hold bias regardless of the arithmetic.

1. The question, answered honestly

The brief asks whether to sell before expiry or renew. The verified evidence says the financial ranking of those two options is inside the measurement error. The base case is separated by $0.13M on a $5.69M base — roughly 2.3% — and the sign depends on which of two official net-lease yields you pick. A decision that flips on a 50-basis-point input is not a decision the model can make for you.

What the model can do is tell you which actions dominate regardless of which way the coin lands. That is what the recommendation above is.

2. Why not just sell

  • You would be selling the invisible asset. Proposition 13 caps the annual assessed-value increase at 2% while area consumer prices rose 3.7% over the last twelve months. That gap is a permanent, non-transferable benefit of holding, and it is not in the price a buyer pays. S14 ↗ S01 ↗
  • You would be selling before the reset. The 2030–2035 window is leverage. A market reset plus term produces the highest-priced version of this asset; selling first gives that value to the buyer.
  • The transaction cost is real but not disqualifying. City and county transfer taxes total about 0.56%; the Measure ULA surtax of 4% only bites above roughly $5.4M of price. At the modelled rent the asset is around $2.31M, well under the threshold. S15 ↗ S16 ↗

3. Why not just renew and forget it

  • One tenant, one parcel, one metro. The tenant reported system same-store sales of -1.1% and closed 17 restaurants in the latest quarter, with negative stockholders' equity. That is a credit exposure, not a bond. S09 ↗ S08 ↗
  • The hazard tail is not symmetric. The USGS puts a 60% chance of an M6.7+ earthquake in the Los Angeles region within 30 years. Across 20,000 simulated paths, the single-asset hold carries a 58% 99th-percentile loss; the diversified path carries 21%. S23 ↗
  • Doing nothing is still a choice — and it is the choice that keeps both risks fully concentrated.

4. The action plan

WindowActionWhy now
0–60 daysAssemble the eight owner facts (P01–P08): rent, exact expiry, escalations, renewal options, basis, debt, recovery structure, parcel geometry. Nothing here is decision-grade until these exist. They are published as blank rows on Verified facts rather than guessed.
0–90 daysCommission parcel-level hazard and insurance verification: FEMA flood zone, CAL FIRE FHSZ class, CGS seismic zone, and a live property quote. An uninsurable parcel overrides the entire financial ranking. This is the cheapest information in the file.
60–180 daysOpen the extension conversation. Ask for a fair-market reset with a floor, a 10-year term, and a guaranty. Test any offer against a 7.0% replacement yield. The tenant reprices renewals to market anyway and needs term; you need the reset. The interests align better than the negotiation feels.
120–240 daysObtain an appraisal and a broker opinion of value. Converts the assumption-based cap rate into an observed one, which is the single input that decides sell versus hold.
If sellingEngage a qualified intermediary before listing, identify replacement property within 45 days, close within 180 days, and buy at least three properties. The 45/180-day clock is statutory and unforgiving; three assets is what converts the tail from 58% to 21%. S17 ↗
If holdingHold a reserve outside the property of roughly 12 months of net operating income, and re-test the decision every 24 months. The simulation's median path has no hazard loss at all; the loss arrives in the tail. A reserve is what turns a tail event into an inconvenience.

6. What this analysis cannot tell you

It cannot tell you the value of this building. It does not know the rent, the exact expiry date, the basis, the debt, or whether the parcel can be insured. It cannot predict interest rates or Los Angeles rents in 2033. What it can do is name the inputs that decide the question, put a number on how sensitive the answer is to each, and tell you which action is robust across all of them. That is what it has done.
